B1 Prep

B1 Intermediate

Practice exercises

Grammar and vocabulary practice, reading tasks in the style of Cambridge B1 Preliminary, and writing tasks. Do a set, then press Check answers to see your score and why each answer is right.

Grammar

Grammar 1
Narrative tenses
Past simple, past continuous, past perfect and used to. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 2
Present perfect simple and continuous
have done or have been doing?; still, yet, already, for, since; present perfect or past simple? 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 3
Conditionals
Zero, first and second conditionals; unless, when, as soon as, in case. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 4
The passive
Present and past passive, passives in other tenses, active or passive? 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 5
Modal verbs
Obligation, advice, possibility and deduction: must, have to, should, might, can't… 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 6
-ing or to-infinitive?
Verb patterns, prepositions + -ing, purpose, and stop/remember/try. 5 sets of 10 sentences.

Reading · B1 Preliminary style

Part 1
Short texts
Notices, messages and emails. 5 sets of 5 texts, one question each.
Part 2
Matching
Five people and eight short texts: find the best match for each person. 5 sets.
Part 3
Multiple choice
5 articles, each with 5 questions on detail, opinion and the writer's purpose.
Part 4
Gapped text
5 articles. Put 5 missing sentences back; three are extra.
Part 5
Multiple-choice cloze
5 texts with 6 gaps. Choose the word that fits: collocations, prepositions, fixed phrases.
Part 6
Open cloze
5 texts with 6 gaps. Write one word: prepositions, pronouns, auxiliaries, linking words.

Writing

Parts 1 & 2
Writing tasks
5 emails (Part 1), and 4 articles and 4 stories (Part 2), about 100 words each, with a word counter and checklist.
