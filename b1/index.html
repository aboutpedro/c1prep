Grammar

Each topic has 5 sets of 10 sentences.

Grammar 1
Narrative tenses
Past simple, past continuous, past perfect and used to. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 2
Present perfect simple and continuous
have done or have been doing?; still, yet, already, for, since; present perfect or past simple? 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 3
Conditionals
Zero, first and second conditionals; unless, when, as soon as, in case. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 4
The passive
Present and past passive, passives in other tenses, active or passive? 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 5
Modal verbs
Obligation, advice, possibility and deduction: must, have to, should, might, can't… 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 6
-ing or to-infinitive?
Verb patterns, prepositions + -ing, purpose, and stop/remember/try. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 7
used to, be used to and get used to
Past habits, being used to something and getting used to it; base verb or -ing? 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 8
Reported speech
say or tell; tense changes; reported questions; requests and orders. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 9
Relative clauses
who, which, that, whose, where, when; defining and non-defining clauses. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 10
Future forms
will, going to, present continuous and simple; will be doing, will have done. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 11
Question tags and indirect questions
It’s cold, isn’t it? Could you tell me where the station is? 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 12
Third conditional and wishes
If I had known, I would have…; I wish, if only. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 13
Quantifiers
both, either, neither; all, every, each; few, a few; no, none. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 14
Comparing; so, such, too and enough
much cheaper, not as… as, the more… the better; so, such, too, enough. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 15
Linking words
although, however, despite; so that, in order to; because of, whereas. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 16
have something done
I had my hair cut. We’re having the kitchen painted. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 17
should have, could have, must have
Regrets, criticism and guesses about the past. 5 sets of 10 sentences.

Vocabulary

Each topic has 5 sets.

Vocabulary 1
Topic vocabulary
Work, the environment, technology, health and fitness, entertainment. 5 word-bank sets.
Vocabulary 2
Collocations
make or do, pay attention, keep a secret, heavy rain… 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 3
Phrasal verbs
Relationships, travel and everyday life: get on with, put off, set off… 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 4
Prepositions
interested in, depend on, the reason for, on purpose, by Friday… 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 5
Topic vocabulary 2
Education, house and home, food and eating out, shopping and services, clothes. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 6
Topic vocabulary 3
Sport and leisure, town and countryside, nature and weather, feelings, communication. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 7
Word formation
Nouns, adjectives and opposites: discussion, kindness, careless, impossible. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 8
Easily confused words
remember or remind, rob or steal, rise or raise, actually or currently. 5 sets of 10 sentences.

Reading · B1 Preliminary style

Part 1
Short texts
Notices, messages and emails. 5 sets of 5 texts, one question each.
Part 2
Matching
Five people and eight short texts: find the best match for each person. 5 sets.
Part 3
Multiple choice
5 articles, each with 5 questions on detail, opinion and the writer's purpose.
Part 4
Gapped text
5 articles. Put 5 missing sentences back; three are extra.
Part 5
Multiple-choice cloze
5 texts with 6 gaps. Choose the word that fits: collocations, prepositions, fixed phrases.
Part 6
Open cloze
5 texts with 6 gaps. Write one word: prepositions, pronouns, auxiliaries, linking words.

Writing

Parts 1 & 2
Writing tasks
5 emails (Part 1), and 4 articles and 4 stories (Part 2), about 100 words each, with a word counter and checklist.

Games

Game
Sentence scramble
Put the words in order to make a sentence. 10 sentences a round.
Game
Memory pairs
Turn over the cards and match each phrasal verb with its meaning.
Game
Odd one out
Four words, and one doesn’t belong. Find it, then see why.
Game
Word of the day
Guess the five-letter word in six tries. A new word every day.
Game
Error hunt
Each sentence has one wrong word. Find it, then fix it.
Game
Hangman
Guess the word letter by letter from its clue.
Game
Crosswords
Themed crossword puzzles. Your answers are saved as you go.
Game
Escape room
Five locked doors and a timer. Solve each puzzle to open the next one.
