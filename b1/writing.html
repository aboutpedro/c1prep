Writing · Parts 1 and 2

Writing tasks

In Cambridge B1 Preliminary you write an email (Part 1) and then choose an article or a story (Part 2). Write about 100 words for each. Your text stays in this browser as you type, so you can come back to it later.

Part 1 · Email (compulsory)

Read this email from your English friend, Jamie, and the notes you have made.

Hi! I'm so excited that I'm coming to visit you next month! [Great!] When's the best time for me to come – at the beginning or the end of the month? [Tell Jamie] I'd love to do something outdoors while I'm there. What do you suggest? [Suggest…] Should I bring anything special? [Yes, explain…]
See you soon!
Jamie

Write your email to Jamie, using all the notes. Write about 100 words.

Read this email from your English teacher, Mr Hall, and the notes you have made.

Dear students,
At the end of term, our class is going on a day trip. We can either visit the science museum or go to the beach. [Say which and why] Would you prefer to travel there by train or by coach? [Tell Mr Hall] We'd also like to have lunch together. What do you think we should do for lunch? [Suggest…] Finally, can anyone help me to organise the trip? [Yes – offer to…]
Best wishes,
Mr Hall

Write your email to Mr Hall, using all the notes. Write about 100 words.

Read this email from your English friend, Kim, and the notes you have made.

Hi! I've got two tickets for a concert next Saturday – it's my favourite band! Would you like to come with me? [Yes!] It starts at 8 pm. How shall we get there? [Suggest…] We could have something to eat before the concert. What kind of food do you like? [Tell Kim] My parents say I have to be home by midnight. Is that OK for you? [Explain…]
Kim

Write your email to Kim, using all the notes. Write about 100 words.

Read this email from Ms Evans, the manager of your sports centre, and the notes you have made.

Dear member,
Thank you for joining Riverside Sports Centre. Which of our activities are you most interested in? [Tell her] What time of day would you prefer to do them? [Say when and why] We are also planning to open a café. What would you like it to sell? [Suggest…] Finally, would you be interested in helping at our open day in June? [Yes / No, because…]
Best regards,
Sandra Evans

Write your email to Ms Evans, using all the notes. Write about 100 words.

Read this email from Alex, an exchange student who is coming to your school, and the notes you have made.

Hi! I'm going to be at your school for three months from September, and I'm really looking forward to it! [Me too!] What's the school like? [Describe…] What do students usually do after school? [Tell Alex] I'm a bit worried about the weather. What clothes should I bring? [Advise…]
Thanks!
Alex

Write your email to Alex, using all the notes. Write about 100 words.

Part 2 · Choose one task

Article. You see this announcement on an English-language website:

Articles wanted: My favourite place to relax

Where do you go to relax? What do you do there? Why is it special to you? Write an article answering these questions, and we will put the best ones on our website.

Write your article in about 100 words.

Article. You see this announcement in an English-language magazine:

Articles wanted: A person I admire

Tell us about someone you admire. It can be someone you know or someone famous. Who is this person? What have they done? Why do you admire them?

Write your article in about 100 words.

Article. You see this announcement on an English-language website for teenagers and young adults:

Articles wanted: Technology and me

Which piece of technology could you not live without? How do you use it? How would your life be different without it?

Write your article in about 100 words.

Article. You see this announcement in your school's English-language magazine:

Articles wanted: A great day out

Where is the best place for a day out in your area? What can you do there? What kind of people would enjoy it?

Write your article in about 100 words.

Story. Your English teacher has asked you to write a story. Your story must begin with this sentence:

When I opened the door, I couldn't believe my eyes.

Write your story in about 100 words.

Story. Your English teacher has asked you to write a story. Your story must begin with this sentence:

Ana looked at her phone and saw the message she had been waiting for.

Write your story in about 100 words.

Story. Your English teacher has asked you to write a story. Your story must begin with this sentence:

It was the first day of the holidays, and it was raining.

Write your story in about 100 words.

Story. Your English teacher has asked you to write a story. Your story must begin with this sentence:

Tom heard a strange noise coming from the garden.

Write your story in about 100 words.

Checklist ticks are for your own review. They don't mark the text automatically.
