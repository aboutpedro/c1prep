Adjective or adverb?

Adjectives describe nouns, and come after be and sense verbs (look, sound, smell, taste, feel). Adverbs describe how we do things. Most adverbs add -ly, but not all.

  • She’s a careful driver. She drives carefully.
  • You look tired.After look: an adjective.
  • He works hard. He hardly works.hardly = almost not.
  • I was bored. The film was boring.

Careful: good → well; fast, hard, late and early don’t change. Lately means recently.
