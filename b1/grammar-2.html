Present simple or present continuous?

The same idea as with the perfect forms: the simple is for habits, permanent situations and states; the continuous is for activities in progress, temporary situations and changes.

  • I work in a hotel, but this month I’m working in the restaurant.My job in general / a temporary change.
  • Prices are going up.A situation that is changing.
  • He’s always losing his keys!always + continuous: a habit that annoys you.

Careful: some verbs are states with one meaning and actions with another: I think you’re right (an opinion) / I’m thinking about it; I have a car / I’m having lunch; It tastes good / I’m tasting the soup.
