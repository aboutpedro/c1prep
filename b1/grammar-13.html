Articles with countable and uncountable nouns

Uncountable nouns never take a or an and have no plural. Use some or a piece of, or no article for things in general; use the for a particular one.

  • Can I give you some advice? / a piece of adviceNot: an advice, advices.
  • Water is essential. / The water in this bottle is cold.
  • She’s looking for work. / She’s got a job.

Careful: these nouns are uncountable in English: advice, information, news, furniture, luggage, weather, work, homework, bread, money.
