The B1 Preliminary exam

Checked against Cambridge English’s own pages on 26 November 2026

B1 Preliminary shows that you can manage in English in everyday life, at work and when you travel: read simple articles, write an email or a story, follow a conversation and give your opinion. It has four papers. Its old name, PET, is still widely used.

Speaking

10–12 minutes for two candidates (15–17 for three) · 25% of your overall score

You take the test with another candidate and two examiners: one asks the questions, the other listens.

Results and the Cambridge English Scale

You get a score for each paper: reading, writing, listening and speaking. Your overall score is the average, so each paper counts for a quarter. All the scores are on the Cambridge English Scale, the same scale for every Cambridge exam (see all five exams on one scale).

ScoreResultCEFR level
160–170Grade AB2
153–159Grade BB1
140–152Grade CB1
120–139Level A2A2
102–119No certificate—

With 140 or more you get the B1 Preliminary certificate, with your grade. With a score one level lower you get a certificate for that level. Scores from 102 to 119 appear on your Statement of Results, without a certificate.
