Practice exercises

Do each task, then press Check answers to see your score, the correct answers and why they are right. Each part has several sets.

Reading and Use of English

Part 1
Multiple-choice cloze
15 sets of 8 gaps. Choose the word that fits: collocations, phrasal verbs, fixed phrases.
Part 2
Open cloze
15 sets of 8 gaps. Write one word: prepositions, linkers, auxiliaries, pronouns.
Part 3
Word formation
16 sets of 8 gaps. Change the word in capitals so it fits the sentence.
Part 4
Key word transformations
16 sets of 6 items. Rewrite the sentence in 3–6 words, using the word given.
Part 5
Multiple choice
6 texts, each with 6 questions on meaning, attitude and detail.
Part 6
Cross-text matching
8 sets of four writers on one topic. Who agrees with whom?
Part 7
Gapped text
6 articles. Put 6 missing paragraphs back; one is extra.
Part 8
Multiple matching
6 sets of four or five short texts and 10 statements.

Writing

Parts 1 & 2
Writing tasks
3 essays and 8 Part 2 tasks, with a word counter and checklist.
All texts and tasks here are original practice material, written in the style of the exam.
