Reading and Use of English

Part 1
Multiple-choice cloze
18 sets of 8 gaps. Choose the word that fits: collocations, phrasal verbs, fixed phrases.
Part 2
Open cloze
18 sets of 8 gaps. Write one word: prepositions, linkers, auxiliaries, pronouns.
Part 3
Word formation
19 sets of 8 gaps. Change the word in capitals so it fits the sentence.
Part 4
Key word transformations
19 sets of 6 items. Rewrite the sentence in 3–6 words, using the word given.
Part 5
Multiple choice
11 texts, each with 6 questions on meaning, attitude and detail.
Part 6
Cross-text matching
11 sets of four writers on one topic. Who agrees with whom?
Part 7
Gapped text
11 articles. Put 6 missing paragraphs back; one is extra.
Part 8
Multiple matching
11 sets of four or five short texts and 10 statements.

Writing tasks

Grammar

Grammar 1
Tenses and aspect
Perfect and continuous aspect, narrative tenses, state verbs used dynamically, will, would and keep for habits. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 2
Future forms and the future in the past
Future continuous and perfect, be to, be about to, be bound to, was going to, would. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 3
Conditionals
Mixed conditionals, inversion (Had I known…, Should you…), but for, supposing, provided, if so, if anything. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 4
Wishes and unreal past
wish, if only, would rather, it’s high time, as if, suppose: the past tense for unreal situations. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 5
The passive and causative
Passives in every form, it is said that, he is thought to have, passive infinitives and -ing, have and get something done. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 6
Reporting verbs
accuse of, insist on, deny -ing, warn against, recommend that he (should)…, reported questions. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 7
Modal verbs
must have, can’t have, needn’t have, ought to have, be bound to, be supposed to, may well, wouldn’t for refusals. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 8
Inversion and emphasis
Never have I…, No sooner… than, Not only…, So… that, Such was…, What I need is…, It was… who. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 9
Verb patterns
-ing or to with a change in meaning, perfect and passive infinitives, make and let, worth -ing, no point in. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 10
Relative and participle clauses
whose, whom, whereby, all of which, which for a whole clause, Having finished…, Built in 1890… 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 11
Comparison and degree
far, by far, nowhere near, every bit as, the more… the better, twice as… as, so, such, too, enough. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 12
Linking words
albeit, much as, even so, notwithstanding, owing to, hence, so as not to, for fear of, be that as it may. 5 sets of 10 sentences.

Vocabulary

Vocabulary 1
Phrasal verbs
rule out, iron out, stem from, play down, come up against, get round to, crop up, cut down on… 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 2
Collocations
bitterly disappointed, highly unlikely, pose a threat, meet a deadline, strike a balance, a foregone conclusion… 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 3
Dependent prepositions
oblivious to, conducive to, wary of, fraught with, refrain from, an insight into, at stake, under threat… 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 4
Word formation
Nouns, adjectives, negative prefixes, verbs and adverbs at C1 level, as in Part 3. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 5
Fixed phrases and idioms
at a loss, by and large, a blessing in disguise, cut corners, call the shots, touch and go… 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 6
Easily confused words
affect or effect, principle or principal, imply or infer, ensure or assure, adverse or averse… (last set Part 1 style) 5 sets of 10 sentences.
