Reading and Use of English · Part 4

Key word transformations

For questions 25–30, complete the second sentence so that it has a similar meaning to the first sentence, using the word given. Do not change the word given. You must use between three and six words, including the word given. Contractions (e.g. didn't) count as two words. Each answer is worth 2 marks; here an answer counts only if it is completely right.
