Reading and Use of English · Part 4

Key word transformations

For questions 25–30, complete the second sentence so that it has a similar meaning to the first sentence, using the word given. Do not change the word given. You must use between three and six words, including the word given. Contractions (e.g. didn't) count as two words. Each answer is worth 2 marks; here an answer counts only if it is completely right.

You rewrite a sentence so that it means the same, using the word given and between three and six words. Each item tests a grammar structure and a piece of vocabulary together, and is worth 2 marks (one for each part).

How to do it

  1. Read both sentences and underline what the second sentence starts and ends with. Your answer must join them.
  2. Ask what the key word forces you to use: a passive? a phrasal verb? an expression (take part in)? a comparison?
  3. Check that nothing is lost or added: same tense, same time, same positive or negative meaning, same degree (quite, very).
  4. Count the words. Contractions count as two words (didn’t = did not).
  5. Never change the key word: not its form (BEING → been), not its spelling.

Watch out for

  • Leaving out part of the original: if the first sentence says yesterday or the company, the meaning must still be there.
  • Writing too many words. If you need more than the limit, the structure is probably wrong.
  • A partly right answer can still get 1 mark, so always write something.

In the C1 Advanced exam, the whole Reading and Use of English paper takes 90 minutes. Aim for about 10 minutes on this part.
