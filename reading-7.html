Reading and Use of English · Part 7

Gapped text

Six paragraphs have been removed from the article. Choose from the paragraphs A–G the one which fits each gap (41–46). There is one extra paragraph which you do not need to use.

Six paragraphs have been taken out of a text and you put them back. There is one extra that you don’t need. The task tests how a text holds together: references, time, the order of ideas, and the links between paragraphs.

How to do it

  1. Read the main text first, all the way through, skipping the gaps, to follow the story or argument.
  2. Read the missing paragraphs and note what each one is about.
  3. At each gap, read the text before and after it. The right answer must link to both sides.
  4. Look for links: pronouns (this, they, such a), names, time words (later, by then), contrast words (however, yet), repeated ideas in different words.
  5. Do the easy gaps first. At the end, check the extra one really doesn’t fit anywhere, and read the whole text again in order.

Watch out for

  • A paragraph with the right topic but the wrong link: he must refer to a man just mentioned, this problem to a problem just described.
  • Time and order: an event can’t be mentioned as past before it happens in the story.
  • Changing an answer you were sure of to make a hard gap work: check the whole text again instead.

In the C1 Advanced exam, the whole Reading and Use of English paper takes 90 minutes. Aim for about 15 minutes on this part.
