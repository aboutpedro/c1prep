Grammar

can and can’t

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

We use can to say that we are able to do something, and to ask for help or permission: I can swim. Can you help me? Can I sit here?

The forms

+I / you / he / she / we / they can swim.
–I / you / he / she / we / they can’t swim.
?Can you swim? – Yes, I can. / No, I can’t.

Careful: can is the same for everyone (no cans), and the next verb has no -s and no to: She can play the piano.

What we use it for

  • I can speak three languages. Fish can’t fly.ability
  • Can you open the door, please? – Sure!asking for help
  • Can I use your phone? – Yes, of course.asking for permission
  • You can’t park here.it’s not allowed
