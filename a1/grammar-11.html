Present continuous

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

We use the present continuous for actions happening now: I’m reading. It’s raining.

am / is / are + -ing

+I’m working. She’s working. They’re working.
–I’m not working. He isn’t working. We aren’t working.
?Are you working? – Yes, I am. Is it raining? – No, it isn’t.

Spelling: make → making (no e) · swim → swimming, run → running, sit → sitting (double the last letter) · study → studying.

Present simple or present continuous?

  • I walk to school every day.a habit: present simple
  • Look! It’s snowing.now: present continuous
  • What are you doing at the moment?now, at the moment
