Grammar

Grammar 1
The verb be
I am, you are, she is; isn’t and aren’t; Are you…? Yes, I am. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 2
Pronouns and possessives
I or me, my or your, Tom’s, Whose…? 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 3
Plurals; this, that, these, those
books, boxes, children; this book, those shoes; two big dogs. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 4
a, an and the
a or an?; a or the?; a/an or no word. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 5
have got and has got
I’ve got a sister. Has she got a car? We haven’t got time. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 6
Present simple: positive and negative
I work, she works; don’t and doesn’t; I like cooking. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 7
Present simple: questions
Do you…? Does he…? Yes, I do. Where do you live? 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 8
Question words
what, where, who, when, how, how old, how many, what time. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 9
Adverbs of frequency and time phrases
always, usually, never; every day, on Mondays, in the morning. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 10
can and can’t
I can swim. Can you help me? Can I open the window? 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 11
Present continuous
I’m reading. Is it raining? Present simple or continuous? 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 12
there is, there are; some and any
There’s a bank. Are there any shops? some milk, any eggs. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 13
Prepositions of place and time
in, on, under, next to; at 7 o’clock, on Monday, in July. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 14
Imperatives, let’s and would like
Sit down. Don’t run. Let’s go. I’d like a tea. Would you like…? 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 15
Past simple: was and were
I was at home. Were you tired? It wasn’t easy. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 16
Past simple: regular and irregular verbs
worked, went, saw; didn’t go; Did you…? 5 sets of 10 sentences.
