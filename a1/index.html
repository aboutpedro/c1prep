Practice exercises

Grammar and vocabulary practice for beginners. Do a set, then press Check answers to see your score and why each answer is right.

Grammar

Each topic has 5 sets of 10 sentences.

Grammar 1
The verb be
I am, you are, she is; isn’t and aren’t; Are you…? Yes, I am. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 2
Pronouns and possessives
I or me, my or your, Tom’s, Whose…? 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 3
Plurals; this, that, these, those
books, boxes, children; this book, those shoes; two big dogs. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 4
a, an and the
a or an?; a or the?; a/an or no word. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 5
have got and has got
I’ve got a sister. Has she got a car? We haven’t got time. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 6
Present simple: positive and negative
I work, she works; don’t and doesn’t; I like cooking. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 7
Present simple: questions
Do you…? Does he…? Yes, I do. Where do you live? 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 8
Question words
what, where, who, when, how, how old, how many, what time. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 9
Adverbs of frequency and time phrases
always, usually, never; every day, on Mondays, in the morning. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 10
can and can’t
I can swim. Can you help me? Can I open the window? 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 11
Present continuous
I’m reading. Is it raining? Present simple or continuous? 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 12
there is, there are; some and any
There’s a bank. Are there any shops? some milk, any eggs. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 13
Prepositions of place and time
in, on, under, next to; at 7 o’clock, on Monday, in July. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 14
Imperatives, let’s and would like
Sit down. Don’t run. Let’s go. I’d like a tea. Would you like…? 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 15
Past simple: was and were
I was at home. Were you tired? It wasn’t easy. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 16
Past simple: regular and irregular verbs
worked, went, saw; didn’t go; Did you…? 5 sets of 10 sentences.

Vocabulary

Each topic has 5 sets of 10 sentences.

Vocabulary 1
People and family
Family, describing people, the body, jobs, countries and nationalities. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 2
Numbers, dates and time
Numbers, first/second, days and months, telling the time, prices. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 3
Home and everyday things
Rooms, furniture, the kitchen, things in your bag, jobs at home. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 4
Food and drink
Food, drinks, meals, at the café, a bottle of, a packet of. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 5
Daily routine and free time
get up, have breakfast; hobbies; play, go or do; school subjects. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 6
Town and transport
Places in town, shops, by bus, turn left, at the hotel. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 7
Clothes, colours and the weather
Clothes, colours, shopping for clothes, the weather and seasons. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 8
Everyday English
Hello and goodbye, in the classroom, please and sorry, opposites. 5 sets of 10 sentences.

Games

Game
Sentence scramble
Put the words in order to make a sentence. 10 sentences a round.
Game
Memory pairs
Turn over the cards and match each phrasal verb with its meaning.
Game
Odd one out
Four words, and one doesn’t belong. Find it, then see why.
Game
Word of the day
Guess the five-letter word in six tries. A new word every day.
Game
Error hunt
Each sentence has one wrong word. Find it, then fix it.
Game
Hangman
Guess the word letter by letter from its clue.
Game
Crosswords
Themed crossword puzzles. Your answers are saved as you go.
Game
Escape room
Five locked doors and a timer. Solve each puzzle to open the next one.
