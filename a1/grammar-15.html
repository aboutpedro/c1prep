Past simple: was and were

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

Was and were are the past of am / is / are: I’m at home now. I was at home yesterday.

was and were

I / he / she / ityou / we / they
+I was tired.We were tired.
–It wasn’t easy.They weren’t at home.
?Was she angry? – Yes, she was.Were you at the party? – No, I wasn’t.

The basics

  • Where were you last night?
  • How was your holiday? – It was great!
  • I was born in 2001.we always say was / were born

Look for the time words: now, today → am / is / are · yesterday, last week, in 2010 → was / were.
