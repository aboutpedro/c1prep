Grammar

Present simple: questions

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

To make a question in the present simple, start with Do or Does: Do you like pizza? Does she speak French?

Do or Does?

questionyesno
Do you / they work here?Yes, I / they do.No, I / they don’t.
Does he / she / it work here?Yes, he does.No, she doesn’t.

Careful: after does, the verb has no -s: Does she play…? (not Does she plays). And Does he have…? (not has).

With question words

The order is: question word + do / does + person + verb.

  • Where do you live?
  • What time does the film start?
  • How does she go to work? – By bus.
  • Why don’t you like cats?
