Past simple: regular and irregular verbs

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

We use the past simple for finished actions in the past: I watched a film last night. We went to the beach on Sunday.

Regular verbs: -ed

most verbs: + edwatch → watched · play → played · visit → visited
-e: + dlive → lived · dance → danced · like → liked
consonant + y → iedstudy → studied · cry → cried
short verbs: doublestop → stopped · plan → planned

Irregular verbs

Many common verbs are irregular. Learn them one by one:

go → wenthave → hadsee → saw
come → camemake → madebuy → bought
eat → atetake → tookget → got
write → wrotemeet → metfind → found

didn’t and Did…?

For negatives and questions we use didn’t and did + the verb with no -ed.

  • I didn’t go out.not didn’t went
  • Did you see Tom yesterday? – Yes, I did. / No, I didn’t.
  • Where did you go on holiday?
