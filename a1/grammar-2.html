Pronouns and possessives

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

Pronouns are short words for people and things: This is Maria. She is from Peru. The word changes with its place in the sentence.

The basics

  • She calls me every day.she before the verb, me after it
  • These flowers are for you. Come with us!after for, with, to…
  • Is this your bag? – Yes, it’s mine.

Tom’s bike

To say who something belongs to, add ’s to the person: Tom’s bike = the bike of Tom.

  • This is my sister’s room.
  • The children’s toys are in the box.children has no -s, so add ’s
  • My parents’ house is big.plural with -s: add only ’

Whose…?

  • Whose coat is this? – It’s Ana’s.
  • Whose keys are these? – They’re mine.

Careful: his is for a man, her is for a woman: Mr Davis and his car. Kate and her parents.
