Grammar

a, an and the

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

We use a or an with one thing: a book, an apple. We use the when we know which one: the moon, the door of this room.

a or an?

Listen to the first sound of the next word.

a + consonant sounda book · a big city · a university (you-ni…)
an + vowel soundan apple · an old house · an hour (the h is silent)

The basics

  • She’s a nurse. He’s an engineer.jobs: a / an
  • I like dogs.plural, in general: no a / an
  • My friend is Paula. I live in Brazil.names, cities, countries: no a / an

a / an or the?

  • I’ve got a cat. The cat’s name is Kiki.first time: a; then we know which cat: the
  • Close the door, please.the door of this room
  • Look at the moon!there is only one

Phrases to learn

  • at home · at work · go to school · by bus · watch TV · have breakfastno article
  • play the guitar · listen to the radio · go to the cinemawith the
  • play footballsports: no article
