Grammar

Question words

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

Question words go at the start of the question. Each one asks about something different.

The question words

Whata thingWhat’s your name? – Sofia.
Wherea placeWhere do you live? – In Lisbon.
Whoa personWho is that? – My uncle.
Whena day, a month, a timeWhen is your birthday? – In March.
What timea clock timeWhat time is it? – Six o’clock.
Howthe way, how you feelHow do you go to school? – By bus.
Whya reasonWhy are you sad? – Because…
Whicha choiceWhich colour, red or blue?
Whosewho it belongs toWhose bag is this? – Ana’s.

How + another word

  • How old are you? – I’m twenty.age
  • How many brothers have you got? – Two.things we can count
  • How much is this T-shirt? – £12.price, or things we can’t count: how much water
  • How often do you go swimming? – Twice a week.how many times
