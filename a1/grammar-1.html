The verb be

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

Be is the most important verb in English. We use it to say who we are, where we are, how old we are and how we feel: I’m Ana. I’m 20. I’m from Brazil. I’m happy.

The basics

  • I’m a student.
  • My brother is twelve.my brother = he
  • My shoes are new.my shoes = they
  • We aren’t ready. It isn’t cold.

Careful: we say I’m not, never I amn’t. And it’s (= it is) is different from its (= of it): The dog is hungry. Its bowl is empty.

Questions and short answers

In questions, am / is / are comes first.

questionyesno
Are you a student?Yes, I am.No, I’m not.
Is she your sister?Yes, she is.No, she isn’t.
Are they from Italy?Yes, they are.No, they aren’t.

With question words

  • What is your name? = What’s your name?
  • Where are you from? – I’m from Mexico.
  • How old is your brother? – He’s sixteen.

Careful: in a short answer with yes, don’t use the short form: Yes, I am. (not Yes, I’m.)
