Grammar

Adverbs of frequency and time phrases

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

Words like always, usually and never say how often we do something.

How often?

These words go before the main verb, but after am / is / are.

  • I always get up at seven.
  • She never eats meat.
  • He is often tired. I’m always hungry.after be
  • We don’t usually go out on Mondays.after don’t / doesn’t
  • Do you often eat fish?in questions: after the person

Time phrases

everyevery day · every week · every Sunday
once, twice…once a week · twice a month · three times a year
onon Mondays · on Saturday mornings
inin the morning · in the evening · in summer
atat seven o’clock · at night · at the weekend

Careful: time phrases usually go at the end: I go to the gym twice a week.
