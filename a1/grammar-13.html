Grammar

Prepositions of place and time

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

Prepositions are small words that say where or when: The cat is under the bed. The class starts at nine.

Where?

  • My keys are in my bag. There’s a picture on the wall.
  • The bank is next to the café. The café is between the bank and the shop.
  • She lives in Madrid. I’m at home. My office is on the third floor.

Learn these: at home, at work, at school, at the bus stop · on the train, on the bus · in the kitchen, in the garden.

When?

  • The class starts at nine o’clock.
  • We have football on Tuesday. Christmas is on 25 December.
  • My birthday is in June. We go skiing in winter.
  • I can’t sleep at night.but in the morning, in the evening
  • I work from Monday to Friday. Wash your hands before dinner.
