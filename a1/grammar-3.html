Plurals; this, that, these, those

Each set has 15 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

For more than one thing, we usually add -s: one book, two books. Some words are different.

Making plurals

most words: + sbook → books · car → cars · day → days
-s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x: + esbus → buses · glass → glasses · watch → watches · box → boxes
consonant + y → iescity → cities · baby → babies
-f / -fe → vesknife → knives · leaf → leaves
different wordsman → men · woman → women · child → children · person → people · foot → feet · tooth → teeth
no changesheep → sheep · fish → fish

Careful: adjectives never take -s: two big dogs (not bigs). The adjective goes before the noun: a red car.
