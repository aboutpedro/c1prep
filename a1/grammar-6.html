Present simple: positive and negative

Each set has 15 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

We use the present simple for things that are always true and things we do again and again: I work in a bank. She drinks coffee every morning.

he, she, it + -s

I / you / we / theyhe / she / it
I workshe works
they livehe lives
we watchit watches
you studyshe studies
I go / do / havehe goes / does / has

Spelling: -ch, -sh, -s, -x, go, do → -es (watches, washes, goes). Consonant + y → -ies (studies, cries). But vowel + y → -s (plays).

don’t and doesn’t

  • I don’t like fish. They don’t live here.
  • She doesn’t eat meat. It doesn’t rain much.

Careful: after doesn’t, the verb has no -s: He doesn’t speak (not doesn’t speaks).

like, love, hate + -ing

  • I love dancing. She likes swimming.
  • My dad doesn’t like cooking. We hate waiting.
