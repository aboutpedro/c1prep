Comparison and degree

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

C1 comparison is less about forming comparatives and more about saying how big the difference is, and about fixed patterns such as the more…, the better and so… that.

Big and small differences

small differenceslightly, a bit, a little: slightly smaller
big differencemuch, far, a lot, considerably, a great deal, significantly: far harder
with a superlativeby far, easily: by far the best
no differenceevery bit as… as, just as… as: every bit as good as
a big difference, the other waynowhere near as / not nearly as… as: nowhere near as fast as

Careful: very can’t modify a comparative: much better, not very better.

Patterns

  • The more you practise, the better you get.two things change together
  • Petrol is twice as expensive as it was.
  • It’s getting harder and harder to find a flat.
  • It costs no more than £100.= only £100
  • It’s not so much a comedy as a tragedy.= a tragedy rather than a comedy

so, such, too and enough

so + adjective / adverbShe spoke so quickly that I couldn’t follow.
so much / many + nounWe had so much luggage that…
such (a) + adjective + nounIt was such a boring lecture that… Such nice weather!
too + adjective + (for someone) + toIt was far too heavy for me to lift.
adjective + enough + toHe isn’t old enough to vote.

Careful: no a with uncountable or plural nouns: such nice weather, such good friends.
