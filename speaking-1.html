Speaking · Test 1

Speaking test 1

A full C1 Advanced Speaking test, about 15 minutes. Work with a partner or a teacher: one of you reads the examiner’s words, and the timers keep each part to exam length. Tap a picture to see it larger.

Part 1 · Interview2 min

Examiner

First of all, we’d like to know something about you.

  • Where are you from?
  • What do you do there?
  • How long have you been studying English?
  • What do you enjoy most about learning English?
Examiner

Now, let’s talk about the way you spend your time.

The examiner asks each candidate one or two of these.

  • How do you usually relax after a busy day?
  • Is there a skill you would like to learn in the next few years? Why?
  • Do you prefer to plan your weekends or decide at the last minute?
  • How much time do you spend online each day, and is it too much?
  • Who has had the biggest influence on the way you study?
2:00Part 1

Part 2 · Long turn4 min

Candidate A Learning new skills

Examiner

Here are your pictures. They show people learning new skills in different situations. I’d like you to compare two of the pictures, and say why the people might be learning in these ways and how difficult it might be for them. You have about a minute.

Candidate A: Do you prefer working in a team or on your own? Why?

Part 3 · Collaborative task4 min

Examiner

Here are some ways people try to reduce stress in modern life. Talk to each other about how effective these ways might be. You have about two minutes.

How effective might these ways of reducing stress be?

doing regular exercise

spending time in nature

using your phone less

taking up a hobby

talking to friends or family

2:00Discussion
Examiner

Now you have about a minute to decide which of these would be hardest for most people to keep doing.

1:00Decision

Part 4 · Discussion5 min

The examiner asks both candidates some of these questions, which follow on from Part 3.

  • Why do you think so many people feel stressed today?
  • Should employers be responsible for their workers’ stress levels?
  • Some people say a little stress is good for us. What do you think?
  • Are young people under more pressure now than their parents were at their age?
  • How can schools help students who are anxious about exams?
  • Do you think people will have more free time or less in the future? Why?
5:00Part 4
