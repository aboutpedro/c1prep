Grammar

Prepositions of movement

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

These prepositions show movement: where somebody or something goes. They usually come after verbs like go, walk, run, drive, fly, jump.

Which way?

  • The cat jumped into the box. He took his phone out of his pocket.
  • We walked across the road and along the river.
  • The train went through a tunnel. The plane flew over the mountains.
  • Walk past the bank. Go up the hill and down the other side.

get in, get on, get off

a car, a taxiget into (or in) · get out of
a bus, a train, a plane, a bikeget on · get off

Movement or place? She walked into the shop (movement). She’s in the shop (place, no movement).
