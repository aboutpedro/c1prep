Writing · Parts 6 and 7

Writing tasks

In Cambridge A2 Key you write a short email or note (Part 6) and a story about three pictures (Part 7). Your text stays in this browser as you type, so you can come back to it later.

Part 6 · Email or note (25 words or more)

You want to go to the cinema with your English friend, Alex, on Saturday.

Write an email to Alex. In your email:

  • invite Alex to the cinema
  • say which film you want to see
  • say where you can meet.

Write 25 words or more.

You have just moved to a new home.

Write an email to your English friend, Sam. In your email:

  • say where your new home is
  • say what you like about it
  • say when Sam can visit you.

Write 25 words or more.

You borrowed a book from your English friend, Jo, and you have lost it.

Write a note to Jo. In your note:

  • say sorry
  • explain what happened
  • say what you will do.

Write 25 words or more.

You are going to have a birthday party.

Write an email to your English classmate, Chris. In your email:

  • invite Chris to the party
  • say when and where the party is
  • tell Chris what to bring.

Write 25 words or more.

You can't go to your English class tomorrow.

Write an email to your teacher, Ms Taylor. In your email:

  • say you can't come to the class
  • explain why
  • ask what homework you should do.

Write 25 words or more.
