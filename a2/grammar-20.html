Grammar

too, enough and very

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

Very just makes a word stronger. Too means “more than is good”, so there is a problem. Enough means “as much as we need”.

very or too?

  • The film was very good. I loved it.no problem
  • This coffee is too hot. I can’t drink it.a problem
  • He’s too young to drive.too + adjective + to + verb

too much, too many

  • There are too many people here.plural nouns: too many
  • I ate too much chocolate.nouns we can’t count: too much

enough

  • I’m not tall enough to reach the shelf.after an adjective
  • We haven’t got enough money.before a noun
  • Did you sleep enough?after a verb

Tip: too small = not big enough. too cold = not warm enough.
