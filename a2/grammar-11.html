Grammar

Prepositions of time and place

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

Prepositions are short words like in, on, at, next to and before. They tell us when or where something happens. They’re small, but the wrong one sounds strange, so it helps to learn them in groups.

Time: in, on, at

inmonths, years, seasons, parts of the day: in May, in 2010, in winter, in the morning
ondays and dates: on Monday, on 5 June, on my birthday, on Saturday night
atclock times and a few fixed phrases: at 7.30, at night, at the weekend, at Christmas

Careful: no preposition before next, last, this, every, today, tomorrow: See you next Friday, not on next Friday.

Place: in, on, at

ininside a space or an area: in the box, in the kitchen, in the park, in Brazil
onon a surface or a line: on the table, on the wall, on the second floor
ata point, or a place where something happens: at the bus stop, at the door, at work, at school, at home

You might also see

  • I saw it on TV / on the internet, but I read it in a book.
  • She’s at the cinema.She’s there to see a film.
  • The cinema is in the city centre.This is where the building is.

Where is it?

  • The bank is between the café and the cinema.
  • There’s a bus stop opposite my house.on the other side of the road
  • The car park is behind the supermarket.
  • Can I sit next to you?

Movement

  • He jumped into the pool. · She walked out of the room.
  • Walk across the bridge. · A plane flew over the house.

before, after, during, until…

  • Wash your hands before dinner. Let’s go out after lunch.
  • My phone rang during the film.during + a noun
  • We waited for two hours.for + a period of time
  • I moved here three years ago.ago goes after the period of time
  • The shop is open from 9 to 6.

You might also see

  • I’m working until six.I work, and at six I stop.
  • Send me the form by Friday.not later than Friday
