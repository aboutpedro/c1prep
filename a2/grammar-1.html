Present simple and present continuous

Each set has 15 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

be or do?

Use be (am, is, are) with adjectives, ages, places and where someone is from. Use do and does to make questions and negatives with other verbs. Don’t put am, is, are in front of another verb in the present simple.

  • Are you tired? / Do you like tea?
  • She isn’t at home. / She doesn’t work on Fridays.
  • I agree.Not: I am agree.
  • I’m 25.Not: I have 25 years.
