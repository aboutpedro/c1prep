Grammar

-ed and -ing adjectives; adjective + preposition

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

Many adjectives have two forms. The -ed form says how a person feels. The -ing form describes the thing that makes them feel that way.

bored or boring?

how you feelwhat makes you feel it
I’m bored.The film is boring.
She’s interested in art.Art is interesting.
We were tired.The walk was tiring.
I was surprised.The news was surprising.

More pairs

excited / exciting · worried / worrying · embarrassed / embarrassing · disappointed / disappointing · frightened / frightening · confused / confusing · amazed / amazing

Careful: I’m boring means other people find you boring! Usually you want to say I’m bored.
