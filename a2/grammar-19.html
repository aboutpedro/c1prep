Present perfect or past simple?

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

Both tenses talk about the past. The question is: do we say when? If we say or ask exactly when, use the past simple. If the time is “up to now”, use the present perfect.

The main difference

  • I’ve been to Japan three times.in my life up to now: present perfect
  • I went to Japan in 2019.a finished time: past simple
  • Have you ever eaten sushi? – Yes, I had it last month.ask with the present perfect, give details with the past simple

Time words help

present perfectpast simple
ever, never, just, already, yet, for, since, so faryesterday, last week, in 2015, two years ago, when I was a child, When…?
  • I’ve lived here for six years / since 2019.
  • We moved here six years ago.ago always goes with the past simple

Careful: questions with When…? use the past simple: When did you arrive? (not When have you arrived?)
