Grammar

The passive

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

We use the passive when the action is more important than the person who does it, or when we don’t know who did it: My bike was stolen. (We don’t know who stole it.)

Active and passive

In a passive sentence, the object of the active sentence moves to the front and becomes the subject. The verb becomes be + past participle.

present simpleam / is / are + past participle
English is spoken here. These shoes are made in Italy.
past simplewas / were + past participle
The bridge was built in 1890. We were invited.

Careful: you need both parts: The museum is closed on Mondays, not The museum closed or is close.

When do we use it?

The basics

  • Coffee is grown in Brazil.Who grows it? It isn’t important.
  • My wallet was stolen.I don’t know who stole it.
  • I was born in 2005.Always passive.

You might also see

  • The Mona Lisa was painted by Leonardo da Vinci.Use by to say who did it.
  • Where are these phones made? – In China.Questions: be + subject + past participle.
  • The match wasn’t cancelled.Negatives: wasn’t / weren’t / isn’t / aren’t.
