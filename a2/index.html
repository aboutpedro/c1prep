Grammar

Grammar 1
Present simple and present continuous
Positive, negative and questions; habits or now?; state verbs. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 2
Past simple and past continuous
Regular and irregular verbs, negatives and questions, was/were doing. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 3
Present perfect
ever, never, just, already, yet, for and since; present perfect or past simple? 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 4
Talking about the future
going to, will and won't, and the present continuous for plans. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 5
Comparatives and superlatives
-er/-est, more/most, irregular forms, as … as, too and enough. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 6
Modal verbs
can, could, must, have to, don't have to, should. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 7
Articles
a or an?; a, an or the?; the or no article. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 8
Question forms
do, does, did and other auxiliaries; question words; subject questions; prepositions at the end. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 9
Countable and uncountable nouns
a/an or some; some and any; much, many, a lot of; a few and a little. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 10
Pronouns and possessives
I or me, my or mine, Ana’s, myself. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 11
Prepositions of time and place
in, on, at; next to, between, opposite; before, after, until. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 12
there is, there are; something, anyone, nowhere
there is/are, there was/were; some-, any-, no- and every- words. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 13
Adverbs and word order
quick or quickly, good or well; always, often, never and where they go. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 14
Verb + -ing or to + verb
enjoy swimming, want to swim, good at drawing, to for purpose. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 15
Linking words, time clauses and if
and, but, so, because; when, before, until; if + will. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 16
The passive
It’s made in Italy. It was built in 1890. Present and past simple passive. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 17
Relative clauses: who, which, that, where
the man who…, the book that…, the town where… 5 sets of 10 sentences.

Reading · A2 Key style

Part 1
Short texts
Notices, messages and emails. 5 sets of 6 texts, one question each.
Part 2
Matching
Three people write about the same topic. 5 sets of 7 questions.
Part 3
Multiple choice
5 articles, each with 5 questions.
Part 4
Multiple-choice cloze
5 short factual texts with 6 gaps. Choose the word that fits.
Part 5
Open cloze
5 emails with 6 gaps. Write one word: prepositions, pronouns, linking words.

Writing
