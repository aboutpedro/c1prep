Grammar

Articles

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

Articles are the small words in front of nouns: a / an, the, or nothing at all. The choice depends on whether the listener knows which thing you mean, and whether you’re talking about things in general.

a and an: one of many

Use a / an with singular countable nouns when you mention something for the first time, or when it’s one of many and it doesn’t matter which. We also use it for jobs.

The choice between a and an depends on the sound, not the letter: an before a vowel sound, a before a consonant sound.

a + consonant soundan + vowel sound
a car, a big applean apple, an old car
a university, a European city (a “y” sound)an hour, an honest man (silent h)

The basics

  • I’ve got a brother and an older sister.
  • She’s a doctor.Not: She’s doctor.
  • Is there a bank near here?

You might also see

  • I go swimming twice a week.
  • The speed limit is 50 kilometres an hour.
  • What a beautiful day!

the: you both know which one

Use the when the listener knows exactly which thing you mean: because you’ve mentioned it before, because it’s clear from the situation, or because there’s only one.

The basics

  • I bought a shirt and a jacket. The jacket was really expensive.Second mention: now we know which jacket.
  • Can you close the door, please?The door in this room.
  • The sun is very strong today.There’s only one.

You might also see

  • It’s the best film of the year.Superlatives take the.
  • I play the guitar. / Let’s go to the cinema.
  • Have you been to the USA? We sailed across the Atlantic.Some country names, and oceans, seas and rivers.

No article: things in general

Use no article with plural and uncountable nouns when you talk about things in general. Most names of people, cities, countries and streets have no article either.

The basics

  • Dogs are very loyal animals.
  • I don’t drink coffee.
  • Maria lives in Madrid.

Common expressions with no article

  • have breakfast / lunch / dinner
  • go to work / school / bed · be at home
  • go by bus / car / train
  • next week, last year · play football · speak English

You might also see

  • I love music. / I love the music in this film.General music, then the music in one particular film.
  • People are strange. / The people next door are very noisy.

Careful: when you talk about something in general, don’t add the: Life is beautiful, not The life is beautiful.
