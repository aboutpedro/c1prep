Grammar

Suggestions

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

When we suggest an idea for us both, we use Let’s, Shall we, Why don’t we or How about. Watch the verb form after each one.

Making suggestions

Let’s + verbLet’s go to the beach. · Let’s not stay at home.
Shall we + verb?Shall we have pizza tonight? · What shall we do?
Why don’t we + verb?Why don’t we take a taxi?
How about / What about + -ing?How about going for a walk? · What about a coffee?
We could + verbWe could go to the cinema, or we could stay in.

Answering

  • Good idea! · Great! · Yes, let’s. · Yes, why not? · Sounds good!yes
  • I’m not sure. · I’d rather not. · I don’t think that’s a good idea.no, politely
  • Can we make it nine? · What about the Italian place instead?a different idea

Advice: should

  • You look tired. You should go to bed.I think this is right
  • You shouldn’t eat so much sugar.
