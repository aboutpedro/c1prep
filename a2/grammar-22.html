Grammar

Requests, offers and would like

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

These are the everyday phrases we use to ask for help, ask for permission, offer to help and offer things. Polite forms make a big difference in English.

Asking for help and permission

asking for helpCan you help me? · Could you open the window, please? · Would you mind closing the door?
asking for permissionCan I use your phone? · Could I ask a question? · May I come in? · Do you mind if I sit here?

Answers

  • Sure. · Of course. · Go ahead. · No problem.
  • I’m sorry, I can’t. · I’d rather you didn’t.polite ways to say no

Careful: Would you mind + -ing: Would you mind waiting? And the answer No, not at all means “yes, I’ll do it”!

Offers

  • That bag looks heavy. I’ll carry it.a decision now: I’ll
  • Shall I open the window? – Yes, please.
  • Would you like a coffee? – Yes, please. / No, thanks.
  • Would you like to come to my party? – I’d love to!

Careful: Do you like coffee? = in general. Would you like a coffee? = now. After would like, use to + verb: I’d like to book a table.
