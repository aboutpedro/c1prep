Mixed tenses

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

In real conversation we change tense all the time. Ask yourself two questions: when is it (past, present, future)? And is it finished, in progress, or a habit?

Present

  • She works in a bank. We usually have dinner at eight.facts and habits: present simple
  • Shh! The baby’s sleeping.now: present continuous

Careful: some verbs are almost never in the continuous: know, like, love, want, need, understand, believe. I know the answer (not I’m knowing).

Past

  • I lived in Paris in 2019.finished time: past simple
  • I was watching TV when the phone rang.a longer action + a short one
  • I’ve seen that film three times.up to now: present perfect

Future

  • I’m going to visit my aunt this weekend.a plan
  • It’s cold. – I’ll close the window.a decision now
  • We’re meeting Tom at seven.an arrangement
  • The train leaves at 9.15.a timetable
