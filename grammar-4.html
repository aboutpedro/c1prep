Wishes and unreal past

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

When we talk about things we’d like to be different, or that are imaginary, English moves the verb one step back into the past: present → past simple, past → past perfect. This is the “unreal past”.

wish and if only

about…formexample
nowwish + past simpleI wish I knew the answer. I wish it weren’t so cold.
ability nowwish + couldShe wishes she could afford a bigger flat.
the pastwish + past perfectIf only we had left earlier!
someone else’s behaviour, or a change we wantwish + wouldI wish you would stop tapping your pen. I wish it would stop raining.

Careful: don’t use wish + would about yourself or for states: I wish I were taller, not I wish I would be taller.

would rather, would sooner, would prefer

same personwould rather / sooner + verb (no to): I’d rather stay in. I’d rather not talk about it.
another person, nowwould rather + person + past simple: I’d rather you didn’t smoke.
another person, past+ past perfect: We’d rather you had told us.
would prefer+ to-infinitive, or it if + past simple: I’d prefer to walk. I’d prefer it if you asked.

it’s time, as if, suppose

  • It’s high time you got a proper job.you should have done it already
  • It’s time to go. / It’s time we went.
  • She looked at me as though I were mad.unreal: I’m not mad
  • He behaved as if nothing had happened.
  • Suppose you were offered the job – would you take it?

Careful: when something is really likely, as if takes a normal tense: It looks as if it’s going to rain.
