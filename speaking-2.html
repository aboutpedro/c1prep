Speaking · Test 2

Speaking test 2

A full C1 Advanced Speaking test, about 15 minutes. Work with a partner or a teacher: one of you reads the examiner’s words, and the timers keep each part to exam length. Tap a picture to see it larger.

Part 1 · Interview2 min

Examiner

First of all, we’d like to know something about you.

  • Where are you from?
  • What do you do there?
  • How long have you been studying English?
  • What do you enjoy most about learning English?
Examiner

Now, let’s talk about where you live.

The examiner asks each candidate one or two of these.

  • What do you like most about the area where you live?
  • If you could change one thing about your town, what would it be?
  • How do you usually get around your city?
  • Do you think you will live in the same place in ten years’ time?
  • What is a special celebration in your family, and how do you celebrate it?
2:00Part 1

Part 3 · Collaborative task4 min

Examiner

Here are some things that can make a city a good place to live. Talk to each other about how important these things are for the people who live there. You have about two minutes.

How important are these things in making a city a good place to live?

reliable public transport

parks and green spaces

cultural life: museums, theatres, concerts

job opportunities

feeling safe on the streets

2:00Discussion
Examiner

Now you have about a minute to decide which of these a city should spend most money on.

1:00Decision

Part 4 · Discussion5 min

The examiner asks both candidates some of these questions, which follow on from Part 3.

  • Why do so many young people move to big cities?
  • What problems can happen when a city grows too quickly?
  • Should cars be banned from city centres? Why or why not?
  • How important is it for neighbours to know each other?
  • Do you think small towns have a future?
  • Who should decide how a city changes: the government, architects or the people who live there?
