Determiners and quantifiers

Each set has 15 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

Determiners and quantifiers tell us how much or which ones. The key questions: is the noun countable, singular or plural, and is it two things or more than two?

all, whole, every, each

  • All (of) the students passed. / Every student passed.all + plural; every + singular
  • I spent the whole day / all day in bed.
  • Each of the twins has a phone.each for two or more, one by one; each of (never every of)
  • The bus runs every ten minutes.regular intervals
