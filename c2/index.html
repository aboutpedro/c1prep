Reading and Use of English

Part 1
Multiple-choice cloze
10 sets of 8 gaps. Choose the word that fits: collocations, phrasal verbs, fixed phrases.
Part 2
Open cloze
10 sets of 8 gaps. Write one word: prepositions, linkers, auxiliaries, pronouns.
Part 3
Word formation
10 sets of 8 gaps. Change the word in capitals so it fits the sentence.
Part 4
Key word transformations
10 sets of 6 items. Rewrite the sentence in 3–8 words, using the word given.
Part 5
Multiple choice
10 texts, each with 6 questions on meaning, attitude and implication.
Part 6
Gapped text
10 articles. Put 7 missing paragraphs back; one is extra.
Part 7
Multiple matching
10 sets of four or five texts and 10 questions.

Writing

Parts 1 & 2
Writing tasks
5 essays and 8 Part 2 tasks, with a word counter and checklist.

Grammar

Each topic has 5 sets of 10 sentences.

Grammar 1
Perfect and continuous forms
to have left, having spent, had been waiting, will have been married, the best meal I had ever had. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 2
Future forms and the future in the past
be to, be due to, be set to, be bound to, on the verge of, was to have started, would. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 3
Conditionals and alternatives to if
mixed conditionals, Had we known…, Were it not for…, but for, provided, supposing, otherwise. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 4
Likelihood, deduction and obligation
may well, in all likelihood, a foregone conclusion, must have, needn’t have, might as well, try as I might. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 5
Inversion and fronting
Not once did he…, Scarcely had we…, So quietly did he speak…, Strange as it may seem…, Gone are the days. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 6
Participle and verbless clauses
Having been rejected…, Built in 1890…, On reading…, With the exams over…, If in doubt… 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 7
Gerunds and infinitives
remember, regret, try, go on; being told, having seen, to have been invited; no point, can’t help. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 8
Reference, substitution and cohesion
the former and the latter, do so, that of, those who, nevertheless, hence, that said, on the contrary. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 9
Wishes, preferences and the subjunctive
wish, if only, I’d rather you didn’t, it’s high time, recommend that she take, be that as it may. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 10
The passive and causative
is being looked into, is said to have been built, is understood to be, have it done, get someone to do it. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 11
Concession, purpose and reason
Talented though he is, albeit, for all, so as not to, lest, with a view to, in that, hence. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 12
Comparison and degree
nowhere near as, every bit as, by far, not so much… as, the more… the more, utterly, adverb position. 5 sets of 10 sentences.

Vocabulary

Each topic has 5 sets of 10 sentences.

Vocabulary 1
Idioms
a dark horse, over the moon, at the end of your tether, between a rock and a hard place, a bolt from the blue. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 2
Phrases with take, set, come, turn, go and place
take for granted, set a precedent, come to light, turn a blind eye, it goes without saying, fall into place. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 3
Collocations
bitterly disappointed, acutely aware, pose a threat, strike a balance, a foregone conclusion, a pang of guilt. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 4
Word formation
perseverance, irreparable, underrated, misleading, unseasonably, setback, breakthrough: Part 3 style. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 5
Phrasal verbs
fob off, gloss over, bank on, iron out, blow over, fall through, get round to, go back on. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 6
Shades of meaning
glance, glimpse, peer, gaze; mutter, mumble, whine; flaw or fiasco; Part 1 practice. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 7
Register, metaphor and new words
formal or informal, by and large, odds and ends, spread like wildfire, staycation, ghosted. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 8
Prepositional phrases
in the wake of, at odds with, on the verge of, under no obligation, prone to, fraught with, devoid of. 5 sets of 10 sentences.

Games

Game

Game

Game
Hangman
