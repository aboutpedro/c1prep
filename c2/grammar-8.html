Grammar

Reference, substitution and cohesion

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

Cohesion is what makes a text hold together. Writers point back to earlier ideas, replace words to avoid repetition, and link sentences logically. This is tested in the open cloze and the gapped text.

Pointing back and forward

  • We chose the latter. the former / the latter = the first / the second of two
  • Prices rose by 10%, which surprised everyone.which for the whole clause
  • Many people dislike change. This is human nature.
  • questions such as cost and safety · Such were the results that…
  • Here is what I want you to do.pointing forward

Substitution

  • The government said it would cut taxes, and it has done so.
  • The population of Lagos is larger than that of Cairo. Those who have paid may leave.
  • I’m afraid so. I doubt it. If so… If not…
  • Some prefer A; others prefer B. The old one is broken.

Linking sentences

contrastnevertheless · even so · that said · then again · on the contrary
resultas a result · consequently · hence · thus · therefore
additionwhat is more · moreover · furthermore · besides
condition / otherotherwise · in that case · in any case

Careful: on the contrary says the opposite is true; on the other hand adds a different point of view.
