Grammar

Concession, purpose and reason

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

Concession, purpose and reason linkers are tested in every part of Use of English. The key is what follows each one: a clause, a noun, an -ing form or an infinitive.

Concession

+ clausealthough · even though · much as · whereas · while
+ noun / -ingdespite · in spite of · notwithstanding · for all
adjective + as / though + subject + verbTalented though he is… Strange as it may seem…
however / whatever + …However hard she tried… Whatever you decide…
albeit + adjective / adverbHe agreed, albeit reluctantly.

Purpose

  • so as not to wake the baby · in order to work in Rome
  • so that he could catch the train · lest I (should) forgetlest = formal
  • with a view to buying · for fear of being overheard · for the purpose of

Reason and result

  • owing to / due to / thanks to + nounbe due to = be caused by
  • seeing that / seeing as · in that · inasmuch as + clause
  • The roads were icy, hence the delay.hence + noun
  • so tired that… · such a boring film that…
