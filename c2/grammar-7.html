Gerunds and infinitives

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

At C2 the verb patterns themselves are familiar; the marks come from the changes in meaning, the perfect and passive forms and the patterns after adjectives and nouns.

Change in meaning

verb+ -ing+ to + infinitive
remember / forgeta memory of the pasta duty to do
regretsorry about the pastsorry to say now (formal)
tryas an experimentmake an effort
go oncontinue the same thingmove to the next thing
stopend an activitystop in order to do
meaninvolveintend

Perfect and passive forms

  • She hates being told what to do.passive -ing
  • He denied having seen it.perfect -ing
  • I’d like to have been invited.perfect passive infinitive
  • The house needs painting / to be painted.

After nouns, adjectives and objects

  • There’s no point (in) worrying. It’s no use talking. I can’t help feeling…
  • a tendency to exaggerate · the last person to leave
  • She made me do it. → I was made to do it.make / hear / see: to in the passive
  • I saw him cross the road. / I saw her waiting.whole action / part of it
