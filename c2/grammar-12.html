Comparison and degree

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

C2 comparison is about degree: how much bigger, how nearly equal, how completely. It also needs the right modifiers and the right place for adverbs.

Modifying comparisons

a big differencefar / much / considerably / a great deal + comparative · by far / easily + superlative
a small differenceslightly / a little / marginally + comparative · not quite as… as
a big gapnowhere near / not nearly as… as
equalevery bit as… as · just as… as · no more… than
multiplestwice as much as · three times the size of
  • The longer we waited, the more nervous we became.
  • She’s not so much a singer as a performer.

Gradable and ungradable

  • utterly breathtaking · totally ridiculous · fully aware · wholly unexpected
  • immensely grateful · exceptionally talented · fundamentally flawed
  • a touch too salty · rather difficult · somewhat disappointing

Adverb position

  • He didn’t even say goodbye. I can hardly hear you. She still hasn’t replied.
  • He’ll definitely be there. I’ve already / just seen it.
  • She speaks French fluently.never between a verb and its object
  • Frankly, I don’t care.comment adverbs at the start
