The C2 Proficiency exam

Checked against Cambridge English’s own pages on 26 November 2026

C2 Proficiency is Cambridge’s highest-level exam. It shows that you can use English with the precision and flexibility of a very skilled user, in study and at work at the highest levels. It has four papers. Its old name, CPE, is still widely used.

Reading and Use of English

1 hour 30 minutes · 40% of your overall score

53 questions on texts of about 3,000 words in all.

PartTaskQuestionsPractice on this site
Part 1Multiple-choice clozeA text with gaps: choose the right word for each (vocabulary).8Practise
Part 2Open clozeA text with gaps: write one word in each (mostly grammar).8Practise
Part 3Word formationA text with gaps: change the word given into the form that fits.8Practise
Part 4Key word transformationsRewrite a sentence in 3–8 words, including the word given, so that it means the same.6Practise
Part 5Multiple choiceA long text with questions on detail, opinion, attitude and implication.6Practise
Part 6Gapped textSeven paragraphs have been taken out of a text: put them back. One is extra.7Practise
Part 7Multiple matchingOne long text or several short ones: which part says what?10Practise

Parts 1–3 and 7: 1 mark for each correct answer. Part 4: up to 2 marks. Parts 5 and 6: 2 marks.

Listening

About 40 minutes · 20% of your overall score

You hear each recording twice; 30 questions.

PartTaskQuestions
Part 1Multiple choiceThree short recordings of about a minute, two questions on each.6
Part 2Sentence completionOne speaker for 3–4 minutes: complete the sentences with one to three words.9
Part 3Multiple choiceA conversation of 3–4 minutes, with four options for each question.5
Part 4Multiple matchingFive short monologues on one theme and two tasks: match each speaker in both. On the computer, this part has been drag and drop since 1 November 2025: you drag each answer to a speaker.10

1 mark for each correct answer.

Speaking

16 minutes for two candidates (24 for three) · 20% of your overall score

You take the test with another candidate and two examiners: one asks the questions, the other listens.

PartTaskTime
Part 1InterviewAnswer the examiner’s questions about yourself.2 minutes
Part 2Collaborative taskTalk with your partner about pictures: first one question, then a decision you make together.4 minutes
Part 3Long turn and discussionSpeak on your own for about two minutes from a card, then discuss the topic with your partner and the examiner.10 minutes

Practise with a partner or a teacher: Speaking test 1, a full test with the examiner’s words, the pictures and a timer for each part.

Results and the Cambridge English Scale

You get five scores: Reading, Use of English, Writing, Listening and Speaking. Your overall score is their average, so the Reading and Use of English paper counts for two fifths of it. All the scores are on the Cambridge English Scale, the same scale for every Cambridge exam (see all five exams on one scale).

ScoreResultCEFR level
220–230Grade AC2
213–219Grade BC2
200–212Grade CC2
180–199Level C1C1
162–179No certificate—

With 200 or more you get the C2 Proficiency certificate, with your grade. With a score one level lower you get a certificate for that level. Scores from 162 to 179 appear on your Statement of Results, without a certificate.
