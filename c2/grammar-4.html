Grammar

Likelihood, deduction and obligation

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

C2 speakers choose exactly how sure they are. Beyond the modal verbs, there is a wide range of expressions of likelihood, and modal perfects for deductions about the past.

How likely?

certainprobablepossibleunlikely
be bound to · be sure to · a foregone conclusionin all likelihood · may / might well · there’s every likelihood · the odds are thatmay / might / could · might conceivably · there’s a chancehighly unlikely · there’s a slim chance · the odds are against

Deduction about the past

  • It must have rained: the ground is wet.sure it happened
  • She can’t / couldn’t have seen it: she didn’t reply.sure it didn’t happen
  • They may / might have got lost.possible
  • The letter should have arrived by now.expected

Obligation, advice and criticism

  • You needn’t have cooked so much.it wasn’t necessary, but you did
  • You shouldn’t have said that. You could have told me!criticism
  • We were supposed to hand it in yesterday.expected (but maybe didn’t)
  • You might as well come. I can hardly refuse. Try as I might…
