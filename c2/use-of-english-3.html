Reading and Use of English · Part 3

Word formation

For questions 17–24, read the text below. Use the word given in capitals to form a word that fits in the gap. There is an example at the beginning (0).

You change the word in capitals so that it fits the gap: a noun, adjective, adverb or verb, sometimes with a prefix and a suffix (CERTAIN → uncertainty). At C2 the changes are often bigger: internal changes, compounds, less common prefixes.

How to do it

  1. Read the whole text first for the meaning.
  2. At each gap, decide the part of speech you need: after an article → a noun; before a noun → an adjective; after a verb → often an adverb.
  3. Decide if the meaning is positive or negative: the text may need un-, in-, im-, dis-, ir-, mis-.
  4. Check singular or plural for nouns (a few difficulties) and the right form for verbs.
  5. Write the word and check the spelling letter by letter.

Watch out for

  • Two changes in one word: RELY → unreliable, COURAGE → discouraging.
  • Spelling changes inside the word: pronounce → pronunciation, maintain → maintenance, generous → generosity.
  • People or things? employer / employee / employment, critic / criticism.
  • Spelling counts: one wrong letter loses the mark.

In the C2 Proficiency exam, the whole Reading and Use of English paper takes 90 minutes. Aim for about 8 minutes on this part.
