Future forms and the future in the past

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

English has many ways to talk about the future, each with its own shade of meaning: an official arrangement, an expectation, a certainty, or something that is about to happen.

Formal and fixed future forms

formmeaningexample
be toofficial plan; instructionThe minister is to visit China. Staff are to wear badges.
be due toexpected at a timeThe law is due to come into force in May.
be set toexpected (news)Prices are set to rise.
be bound tocertainYou’re bound to make mistakes.
be about to / on the verge ofvery soonThe train is about to leave.

The future seen from the past

  • I was going to call, but I fell asleep.a plan that didn’t happen
  • Little did she know that she would become CEO.
  • The match was to have started at three.was to have + past participle = it didn’t happen
  • We were about to leave when the phone rang.

Continuous and perfect futures

  • This time tomorrow I’ll be lying on a beach.in progress at a future time
  • Will you be using the car tomorrow?polite question about plans
  • By 2040 they will have found a cure.complete before a future time
