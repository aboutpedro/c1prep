Grammar

Inversion and fronting

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

Inversion and fronting move words to the start of a sentence for emphasis or style. They are typical of formal writing and Part 4 transformations.

Inversion after negative and limiting words

Never / Rarely / Seldom / Not onceNot once did he thank me.
No sooner… than / Hardly / Scarcely… whenScarcely had we begun when the lights went out.
Only + time / by / when / thenOnly by working together can we solve this.
Not untilNot until much later did I understand.
On no account / At no time / In no way / NowhereAt no time were the passengers in danger.
LittleLittle did they suspect the truth.

so, such, nor, as

  • So quietly did he speak that nobody heard.
  • Such was the demand that tickets sold out.
  • She didn’t believe him, nor did I. He was a soldier, as was his father.

Fronting and cleft sentences

  • Try as he might, he couldn’t lift it. Strange as it may seem, I enjoyed it.
  • Much as I admire her, I disagree.= although
  • Into the room walked a tall man. Gone are the days when…
  • What I need is a holiday. All I did was ask. It wasn’t until I left that…
