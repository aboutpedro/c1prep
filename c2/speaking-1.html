Speaking · Test 1

Speaking test 1

A full C2 Proficiency Speaking test, about 16 minutes. Work with a partner or a teacher: one of you reads the examiner’s words, and the timers keep each part to exam length. Tap a picture to see it larger.

Part 1 · Interview2 min

Examiner

First of all, we’d like to know something about you.

  • Where are you from?
  • What do you do there?
  • How long have you been studying English?
Examiner

And now some other questions.

The examiner asks each candidate one or two of these.

  • What do you find most rewarding about the work or studies you do?
  • How do you switch off when you’re not working?
  • If you could spend a year doing something completely different, what would it be?
  • How has your idea of success changed as you’ve got older?
2:00Part 1

Part 2 · Collaborative task4 min

Both candidates Work and life

Examiner

Here are some pictures of people at work and at home. First, I’d like you to look at pictures A and D and talk together about how the people might be feeling about their working lives. You have about a minute.

1:00Pictures A and D
Examiner

Now look at all the pictures. These pictures are going to be used in a magazine article about work–life balance. Talk together about what each picture says about the issue. Then decide which picture should go on the front cover. You have about three minutes.

3:00All the pictures

Part 3 · Long turn and discussion10 min

Candidate A Prompt card

How important is it for people to feel that their work has a purpose?

  • financial security
  • personal fulfilment
  • respect from others
Examiner

Candidate A, you have about two minutes to talk about this.

2:00Candidate A

Then the examiner asks:

  • Candidate B: Would you accept a job you found meaningless if it paid very well?
  • Both: Do you think the idea of a “job for life” has disappeared?

Candidate B Prompt card

What makes someone a good leader?

  • the ability to communicate
  • making difficult decisions
  • earning people’s trust
Examiner

Candidate B, you have about two minutes to talk about this.

2:00Candidate B

Then the examiner asks:

  • Candidate A: Are leaders born or made?
  • Both: Should leaders be judged on their results or on their methods?

Both candidates Discussion

  • Some people say we live to work rather than work to live. How far do you agree?
  • Should governments limit the number of hours people can work?
  • How might working from home change the way society is organised?
  • Is ambition always a positive quality?
  • To what extent should a company be responsible for its employees’ wellbeing?
4:00Discussion
