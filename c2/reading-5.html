Reading and Use of English · Part 5

Multiple choice

For questions 31–36, choose the answer (A, B, C or D) which you think fits best according to the text.

A long text with six questions on detail, opinion, attitude, the writer’s purpose, implied meaning, and what a word or phrase refers to. The questions follow the order of the text.

How to do it

  1. Skim the whole text first to get the main idea and the writer’s attitude.
  2. Read the first question stem only (not the options) and find the part of the text it is about.
  3. Read that part carefully and answer the question in your own words before you look at the options.
  4. Choose the option that matches your answer. Check why each of the others is wrong: it says something the text doesn’t, or the text says the opposite, or it is true but doesn’t answer the question.
  5. For questions about a word or phrase, read the sentences before and after it.

Watch out for

  • Options that repeat words from the text but change the meaning. The right option usually paraphrases the text.
  • Your own opinion: answer only from what the writer says or implies.
  • Options that are true in general, but not what the question asks about.

In the C2 Proficiency exam, the whole Reading and Use of English paper takes 90 minutes. Aim for about 18 minutes on this part.
