The passive and causative

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

The passive is used when the action matters more than who did it. At C2 you need every form, including reporting structures, prepositional verbs and causatives.

All the forms

  • The proposal is being considered.present continuous
  • The road will have been finished by May.future perfect
  • She hates being photographed.-ing
  • The problem is being looked into. The children were looked after.keep the preposition

Passive reporting

It + passive + thatIt is said that the island is haunted.
subject + passive + to + infinitiveHe is thought to be armed.
… + to have + past participle (earlier)The castle is said to have been built in 1100.
… + to be + -ing (in progress)He is understood to be negotiating.

have, get and causatives

  • I’m going to have / get my hair cut.someone does it for me
  • He had his car stolen.something bad happened to him
  • I’ll have the technician look at it. Can you get someone to fix it?
  • She got promoted. The window got broken.get + past participle: informal
