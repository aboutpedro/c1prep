Listening · Part 1

Short extracts

You will hear three different extracts. For questions 1–6, choose the answer (A, B or C) which fits best according to what you hear. There are two questions for each extract.

Short recordings, each with its own question. You hear each one twice.

How to do it

  1. Before each recording, read the question and the options, and underline the key idea: is it asking about a feeling, an opinion, a reason, what someone is doing?
  2. On the first listening, get the general situation and choose an answer in pencil.
  3. On the second listening, check your choice and listen for why the other two options are wrong.
  4. Don't stay on a question you've missed: the next recording starts straight away.

Watch out for

  • Options that use words you hear but don't answer the question.
  • Speakers who change their mind (“I thought…, but actually…”): the answer is usually what they think in the end.
  • In conversations, check who says what: the question may ask about one speaker only.

In the C1 Advanced exam, the Listening paper takes about 40 minutes, and you hear every recording twice. You can replay recordings here, but try each set the exam way first.
