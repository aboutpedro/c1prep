Listening · Part 4

Multiple matching

You will hear five short extracts in which people talk about taking up a sport as adults. While you listen, you must complete both tasks. For questions 21–25, choose from the list (A–H) why each speaker took up the sport. For questions 26–30, choose from the list (A–H) what each speaker has gained.

Five people talk about the same subject, and there are two tasks: you choose two things about each speaker, each from its own list. You hear the five speakers twice.

How to do it

  1. Read both lists before the recording starts.
  2. On the first listening, you can do Task 1 and then Task 2, or both at once: try both and see which suits you.
  3. On the second listening, check your answers and fill any gaps.
  4. Each speaker matches one option in each list. The answers to the two tasks can come at any point in what a speaker says.

Watch out for

  • Speakers often mention ideas from other options. Listen for what they really mean.
  • Don't choose an option just because a speaker uses one of its words.

In the C1 Advanced exam, the Listening paper takes about 40 minutes, and you hear every recording twice. You can replay recordings here, but try each set the exam way first.
