Nouns and articles

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

At C1, small words in the noun phrase carry a lot of marks: whether a noun is countable, which article it takes, and whether the verb after it is singular or plural.

Countable or uncountable?

always uncountablecountable or uncountable, with a change in meaning
advice, information, news, research, progress, equipment, furniture, luggage, evidence, knowledge, accommodationexperience (skill) / an experience (an event) · paper (material) / a paper (newspaper, essay) · time / a good time · work / a work of art
  • She gave me some useful advice. / a piece of advicenot an advice, advices
  • The news is good. A lot of research has been done.uncountable + singular verb

Articles beyond the basics

  • The cheetah is the fastest land animal.the + singular noun for a whole species or type of invention
  • We should help the unemployed.the + adjective = a group of people
  • Life in a city is tiring. / The life of a farmer is hard.general: no article; a particular one: the
  • in hospital · at university · by bus · elected presidentset phrases with no article
  • the Netherlands · the UK · the Alps · Mount Everest · Lake Como

Singular or plural verb?

  • The police are investigating.police, people: always plural
  • The committee has / have decided.group nouns: plural is common in British English when we think of the members
  • A number of students have… / The number of students has…
  • Ten kilometres is a long way. Economics is difficult.

’s, of and compound nouns

  • my parents’ car · the children’s toys · Tom and Ana’s flat
  • an hour’s drive · a week’s holidaytime + ’s
  • a ten-minute walk · a five-storey buildingnumber + singular noun before another noun
  • the name of the film · a bus stop · a friend of mine
