Writing · Parts 1 and 2

Writing tasks

In the exam you have 1 hour 20 minutes for both parts: one essay (Part 1) and one task you choose (Part 2). Write 140–190 words for each. Your text stays in this browser as you type, so you can come back to it later.

Part 2 · Choose one task

Article. You see this announcement in an English-language magazine:

Articles wanted: A day I will never forget

Tell us about a day that was special to you. What happened, and why do you still remember it so clearly? The best articles will be published in next month's magazine.

Write your article.

Article. You see this notice on an English-language website:

My favourite place in town

Where do you most like to spend time in your town? What do you do there, and what makes it special? Write us an article and we will publish the most interesting ones.

Write your article.

Email. You have received this email from your English-speaking friend, Alex:

I'm coming to your country for two weeks next summer and I'd love to visit you for a few days! What's the best thing to do in your area? What kind of clothes should I bring? Is there anything else I should know before I come?

Write your email.

Letter. You see this advertisement in an English-language newspaper:

Summer camp assistants wanted

We need friendly, energetic people to help at our summer camp for children aged 8–12. You will help to organise sports, games and evening activities. Good English is essential. Write to us explaining why you would be suitable for the job.

Write your letter of application.

Report. Your English teacher has asked you to write a report about the sports facilities at your school or college. In your report, you should describe the facilities, say what students like about them and suggest how they could be improved.

Write your report.

Report. The tourist office in your town wants to attract more young visitors. You have been asked to write a report describing what the town offers young people at the moment and recommending what could be done to make it more attractive to them.

Write your report.

Review. You see this announcement on an English-language website:

Reviews wanted: A film everyone should see

Tell us about a film that you think everyone should watch. What is it about? Why did you like it so much? What kind of people would enjoy it most?

Write your review.

Review. You see this notice in an English-language magazine:

Where to eat

Have you been to a café or restaurant recently? Write a review for our readers. Tell us about the food, the service and the atmosphere, and say whether you would recommend it.

Write your review.
