Reading and Use of English · Part 7

Multiple matching

For questions 43–52, choose from the sections. The sections may be chosen more than once.

Ten questions and several texts or sections. You find which text says each thing. The questions are written in different words from the texts, so the task tests paraphrase and fast, careful reading.

How to do it

  1. Read the questions first and underline the key idea in each one (not single words).
  2. Read the first text or section and decide which questions it answers. Then move to the next.
  3. When you find a possible match, read that part again carefully: does it say exactly what the question says?
  4. If two texts seem to match, compare the details (all or some? past or now? the writer or someone else?).
  5. Don’t leave anything empty: at the end, make your best guess.

Watch out for

  • Word matching: the same word can appear in a text that doesn’t answer the question. Look for the same idea.
  • Questions about one person’s opinion, a plan or a regret: check who says it, and when.
  • Spending too long on one question. Move on and come back to it.

In the B2 First exam, the whole Reading and Use of English paper takes 75 minutes. Aim for about 12 minutes on this part.
