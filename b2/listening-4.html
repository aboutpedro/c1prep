Listening · Part 4

Interview

You will hear an interview with a voice actor called Mark Ellis. For questions 24–30, choose the best answer (A, B or C).

An interview or conversation of about four minutes, with questions on opinions, attitudes and details. You hear it twice.

How to do it

  1. Read the questions before the recording starts: they come in the same order as the conversation.
  2. Listen for the interviewer's questions: they often tell you when the next answer is coming.
  3. Choose on the first listening, then check on the second, especially why the other options are wrong.

Watch out for

  • Options that repeat words from the recording but change the meaning.
  • Something the speaker mentions but then rejects, or that other people believe.

In the B2 First exam, the Listening paper takes about 40 minutes, and you hear every recording twice. You can replay recordings here, but try each set the exam way first.
