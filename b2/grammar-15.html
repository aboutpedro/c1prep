Questions

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

At B2 you need to ask questions politely and naturally: indirect questions, question tags, questions that end with a preposition, and short answers.

Indirect questions

After Could you tell me…, Do you know…, I wonder…, use normal word order (subject before verb) and no do, does or did.

directindirect
Where is the station?Could you tell me where the station is?
What time does the film start?I wonder what time the film starts.
Is the shop open?Do you know if / whether the shop is open?

Question tags

  • You’re coming, aren’t you? She doesn’t eat meat, does she?positive sentence → negative tag, and the other way round
  • They’ve left, haven’t they? He can swim, can’t he?use the same auxiliary or modal
  • I’m late, aren’t I? Let’s go, shall we? Nobody called, did they?special cases

Question words and prepositions

  • What are you looking at? Who did you go with?the preposition goes at the end
  • What’s she like? What do you think of it?describe her; your opinion
  • Who made this cake? (not Who did make)who is the subject: no do/did

Short answers, so and neither

  • "Did you enjoy it?" "Yes, I did." "Will you be there?" "I’m afraid not."
  • "I’m tired." "So am I." "I don’t eat fish." "Neither do I."so / neither + auxiliary + subject
  • "Aren’t you hungry?" "No, I’m not."a negative question can show surprise
