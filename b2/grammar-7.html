Grammar

Reported speech and reporting verbs

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

Reported speech tells someone what another person said, without quoting their exact words. At B2 the key is choosing the right reporting verb and the pattern that follows it: he denied taking it, she advised me to wait.

Tense changes

When the reporting verb is in the past (said, told), the verb usually moves one step back.

direct speechreported speech
present simple: “I am tired.”past simple: She said she was tired.
present continuous: “We’re moving.”past continuous: They said they were moving.
present perfect / past simple: “I’ve lost / I lost it.”past perfect: He said he had lost it.
will / can / must: “I’ll call.”would / could / had to: He said he would call.

Words for time and place often change too

now → thentoday → that day
tomorrow → the next dayyesterday → the day before
last week → the week beforehere → there, this → that

Careful: say has no person after it; tell needs one. She said (to me) that…, She told me that…, not She said me.

Reported questions

Use statement word order and no do / does / did. For yes/no questions, add if or whether.

  • “Where do you live?” → She asked me where I lived.
  • “Are you coming?” → He asked if I was coming.
  • Could you tell me where the station is?polite indirect questions follow the same rule

Reporting verbs

Reporting verbs summarise what someone meant, not just what they said. Each verb has its own pattern.

verb + toagree, offer, promise, refuse, threaten: He refused to sign.
verb + person + toadvise, ask, encourage, invite, remind, persuade, tell, warn: She advised me to wait.
verb + -ingadmit, deny, recommend, suggest: He denied taking it.
verb + preposition + -ingapologise for, insist on, admit to: She insisted on paying.
verb + person + preposition + -ingaccuse of, blame for, congratulate on, thank for: They accused him of lying.
verb + thatadmit, agree, deny, explain, promise, suggest: She suggested that we left early.

Careful: suggest never takes a person + to. Say She suggested going or She suggested that we went, not She suggested us to go.
