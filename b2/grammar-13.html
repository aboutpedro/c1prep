Articles

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

English uses a/an, the or no article at all, and the choice depends on whether the listener knows which thing you mean, and whether you are talking about things in general.

a, an and the

a / anone of many, or first mention: I stayed in a hotel. Jobs: She’s an engineer. Per: twice a week
thethe listener knows which one: Pass me the salt. Second mention: …but the hotel was noisy.
thethere is only one: the sun, the internet, the best, the only person

Careful: the sound matters, not the letter: an hour, an honest man (silent h), but a university, a European (a "you" sound).

In general: no article

  • Dogs are more loyal than cats.plural nouns in general
  • I love music. Honesty matters.uncountable and abstract nouns in general
  • The dogs next door bark all night.particular dogs: the
  • The rich should pay more.the + adjective = a group of people
