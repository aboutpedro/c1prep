Grammar

Modal verbs

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

Modal verbs show how necessary, possible or certain something is. At B2 you also need their past forms: must have, can’t have, needn’t have, should have.

Obligation and necessity

musta rule, or something the speaker feels is necessary: Visitors must sign in. I must call Mum.
have tonecessary because of a rule or situation: I have to wear a uniform.
mustn’t / can’tnot allowed: You mustn’t smoke here.
don’t have to / don’t need to / needn’tnot necessary, but you can: You don’t have to come.
should / ought toadvice, the right thing to do: You should rest.

Careful: must has no past or future form. Use had to and will have to: I had to work late yesterday.

Ability and permission

couldgeneral ability in the past: I could read when I was four.
was able to / managed toone success on one occasion: Everyone was able to escape.
be able towhere can has no form: I’ll be able to…, I’ve never been able to…
be allowed to / letpermission: We weren’t allowed to use phones. They let us leave early.

Careful: let + person + verb, with no to: They let me stay. But allow takes to: They allowed me to stay.

Deduction: present and past

  • The lights are on. They must be at home.
  • He can’t have seen us. He didn’t say hello.couldn’t have is also possible
  • I might have left it in the taxi.may have / could have mean the same
  • She must have been waiting for hours.continuous: an activity over a period

Careful: the opposite of must for deduction is can’t, not mustn’t: It can’t be true.

needn’t have, didn’t need to, should have

needn’t have + past participleyou did it, but it wasn’t necessary: I needn’t have cooked so much.
didn’t need to + verbit wasn’t necessary (usually, so you didn’t): I didn’t need to take a taxi.
should have / shouldn’t havecriticism or regret about the past: You should have told me. He shouldn’t have driven so fast.
