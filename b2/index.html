Practice exercises

Do each task, then press Check answers to see your score, the correct answers and why they are right. Each part has several sets.

Reading and Use of English

Part 1
Multiple-choice cloze
10 sets of 8 gaps. Choose the word that fits: collocations, phrasal verbs, fixed phrases.
Part 2
Open cloze
10 sets of 8 gaps. Write one word: prepositions, linkers, auxiliaries, pronouns.
Part 3
Word formation
10 sets of 8 gaps. Change the word in capitals so it fits the sentence.
Part 4
Key word transformations
10 sets of 6 items. Rewrite the sentence in 2–5 words, using the word given.
Part 5
Multiple choice
10 texts, each with 6 questions on detail, opinion and attitude.
Part 6
Gapped text
10 articles. Put 6 missing sentences back; one is extra.
Part 7
Multiple matching
10 sets of four or five short texts and 10 questions.

Writing

Parts 1 & 2
Writing tasks
5 essays and 8 Part 2 tasks, with a word counter and checklist.

Grammar

Grammar 1
Present tenses
Present simple and continuous, state verbs, present perfect simple and continuous, for, since, yet. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 2
Past tenses, used to and would
Narrative tenses, past perfect continuous, used to and would, be and get used to. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 3
Future forms
will, going to, future continuous and perfect, time clauses, be about to, be due to. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 4
Conditionals
All four types, mixed conditionals, unless, as long as, in case, otherwise. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 5
Wishes and regrets
wish, if only, should have, would rather, it’s time, had better. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 6
The passive and causative
Passives in all tenses, it is said that, he is said to, have something done. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 7
Reported speech and reporting verbs
Tense changes, reported questions, deny, suggest, accuse, apologise… 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 8
Modal verbs
Obligation, ability, deduction, needn’t have and didn’t need to. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 9
Verb patterns
-ing or to; remember, stop, try, regret; make, let, want someone to. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 10
Relative clauses
Defining and non-defining, whose, whom, all of which, shortened clauses. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 11
Comparison
much, far, slightly, nowhere near; the more…, the better; twice as… as. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Grammar 12
Linking words
Contrast, purpose, reason and result: despite, so as to, due to, such… that. 5 sets of 10 sentences.

Vocabulary

Vocabulary 1
Phrasal verbs
Work and study, relationships, problems and solutions: put off, come up with, take after… 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 2
Collocations
make, do, take and have; heavy traffic, reach a decision, highly unlikely… 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 3
Dependent prepositions
capable of, depend on, the reason for, on purpose, at risk… 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 4
Word formation
Nouns, adjectives, negative prefixes, verbs and adverbs, as in Part 3. 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 5
Fixed phrases and idioms
in the long run, out of the question, a piece of cake, call it a day… 5 sets of 10 sentences.
Vocabulary 6
Easily confused words
say or tell, rise or raise, job or work, sensible or sensitive… 5 sets of 10 sentences.
