Grammar

Linking words

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

Linking words show how ideas connect: contrast, purpose, reason or result. They’re tested in Part 2 of Reading and Use of English and you need them for every Writing task. The key is knowing what grammar follows each one.

Contrast

although / even though / though + clauseAlthough it was raining, we enjoyed it.
despite / in spite of + noun or -ingDespite the rain… In spite of feeling ill…
however / neverthelessstart a new sentence, then a comma: It’s good. However, it’s expensive.
whereas / whiletwo different facts: He loves football, whereas I prefer tennis.

Careful: no of after despite, and no clause: Despite being tired or Despite the fact that he was tired, not despite of or despite he was.

Purpose

to / in order to / so as to + verbShe moved in order to find work.
so as not to / in order not toWe whispered so as not to wake him.
so that + clauseoften with can, could, would: She saved so that she could travel.
for + -ing / nounwhat something is used for: a knife for cutting bread
in case + clauseto be ready for something possible: Take a map in case you get lost.

Careful: for a person’s purpose, use to, not for: I went to the bank to get money, not for get or for getting.

Reason and result

because / as / since + clauseAs it was late, we took a taxi.
because of / due to / owing to + nounThe match was off due to the weather.
so (same sentence)The road was closed, so we took a detour.
as a result / therefore / consequentlylink to the sentence before: He didn’t study. As a result, he failed.
so … that / such … thatso + adjective, such (a) + adjective + noun: so hot that…, such a good film that…

Adding ideas

  • She’s talented as well as hard-working.as well as + noun, adjective or -ing
  • Apart from being cheap, it’s also very central.
  • The hotel was dirty. Moreover, the staff were rude.also: In addition, What’s more, Furthermore
  • On the one hand, it’s a great job. On the other hand, the pay is low.
