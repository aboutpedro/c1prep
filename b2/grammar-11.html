Gradable and extreme adjectives

Gradable adjectives (cold, good, tired) can be stronger or weaker: use very, extremely, fairly, a bit. Extreme adjectives (freezing, fantastic, exhausted, impossible) already mean “very”: use absolutely, completely, totally. Really goes with both.

  • It’s very cold. / It’s absolutely freezing.Not: very freezing.
  • I’m really tired. / I’m really exhausted.

Where adverbs go

  • I always read the news. She is never late.Before the main verb, but after be.
  • I’ve just finished.After the first auxiliary.
  • He speaks French very well.Not between the verb and its object.
