Comparison

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

At B2 it isn’t enough to say bigger or the biggest. You need to show how big the difference is (slightly, far, nowhere near) and use patterns like the more…, the better.

  • My brother isn’t as tall as me.not as … as = less
  • It’s less interesting than her first book.
  • That’s the funniest joke I’ve ever heard.

How big is the difference?

smallslightly, a bit, a little: slightly heavier
bigmuch, far, a lot, considerably, significantly: far better
the most of allby far + superlative: by far the best
the samejust as … as, exactly the same as: just as good as
much lessnot nearly as / nowhere near as: nowhere near as hard as
multiplestwice / three times as … as: twice as big as

Careful: very can’t go before a comparative. Say much better, not very better.

The more…, the better

the + comparative, the + comparative: when one thing changes, the other changes too.

  • The more you practise, the better you get.
  • The sooner we leave, the sooner we’ll arrive.
  • The traffic is getting worse and worse.it keeps changing
  • Flights are becoming more and more expensive.

Other ways to compare

the same as / similar to / different fromYour phone is the same as mine.
like + nounsimilar to: She sings like an angel.
as + nouna job or role: He works as a nurse.
fewer / lessfewer + plural, less + uncountable: fewer people, less time
compared with / toCompared with last year, sales are up.
