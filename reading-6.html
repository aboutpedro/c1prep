Reading and Use of English · Part 6

Cross-text matching

For questions 37–40, choose from the writers A–D. The writers may be chosen more than once.

Four short texts by different writers on the same topic, and four questions. The questions ask which writer shares or disagrees with another writer’s opinion on a particular point.

How to do it

  1. Read each text and write down, in a few words, each writer’s view on each main point.
  2. Read a question and find the point it is about (for example, the cost of a project).
  3. Compare the writers on that point only: who agrees, who disagrees, who has a different reason.
  4. Answer the question exactly: it may ask which writer differs from the others, or which one shares a view with writer A.

Watch out for

  • A writer may agree on one point and disagree on another. Keep the points separate.
  • Mild opinions: not entirely convinced is still disagreement.
  • Mixing up the writers. Your short notes on each writer help here.

In the C1 Advanced exam, the whole Reading and Use of English paper takes 90 minutes. Aim for about 10 minutes on this part.
