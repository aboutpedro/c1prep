Adjectives, adverbs and modifiers

Each set has 10 sentences. Follow the instructions above the sentences, then press Check answers.

Advanced writing and speaking depend on precise adjectives and adverbs: the right modifier for the adjective, the right form of the adverb and the right order.

Order and compound adjectives

Usual order: opinion – size – age – shape – colour – origin – material – purpose + noun.

  • a beautiful old Italian table · a small wooden house
  • a time-consuming task · a well-known writer · a thought-provoking film
  • a three-hour journeynumber + singular noun
