Emphasis and inversion

A short explanation, then exercises from B2 to C2. Each exercise page has at least 5 sets of sentences to complete, a grammar guide, and every answer explained.

To stress part of a sentence, English uses cleft sentences (It was… that, What… is), emphatic do, and inversion after a negative word at the start of a sentence, with the word order of a question.

  • It was Maria who found the key.
  • Never have I seen such a mess.
  • I do like your shirt!
