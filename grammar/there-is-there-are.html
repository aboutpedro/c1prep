there is and there are

A short explanation, then exercises from A1 to A2. Each exercise page has at least 5 sets of sentences to complete, a grammar guide, and every answer explained.

There is (singular) and there are (plural) say that something exists or is in a place. In the past, use there was and there were.

  • There’s a café near here.
  • There are two bedrooms.
  • Is there any milk? No, there isn’t.
