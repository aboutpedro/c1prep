Present perfect

A short explanation, then exercises from A2 to C2. Each exercise page has 5 sets of 10 sentences, a grammar guide, and every answer explained.

The present perfect (have done) connects the past with now: experiences, recent news, and situations that started in the past and are still true. With a finished time like yesterday or in 2019, use the past simple instead.

  • Have you ever been to Lisbon?
  • I’ve lived here since 2020.
  • I’ve been studying all morning.
