Wishes and the unreal past

A short explanation, then exercises from B1 to C2. Each exercise page has at least 5 sets of sentences to complete, a grammar guide, and every answer explained.

After wish, if only, would rather and it’s time, a past form talks about the present or the future, and the past perfect talks about the past. The tense goes one step back because the situation isn’t real.

  • I wish I had more time.
  • If only I hadn’t said that.
  • It’s time we left.
