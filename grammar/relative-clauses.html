Relative and participle clauses

A short explanation, then exercises from A2 to C2. Each exercise page has 5 sets of 10 sentences, a grammar guide, and every answer explained.

Relative clauses (who, which, that, where, whose) add information about a noun. Defining clauses say which one; non-defining clauses, between commas, add extra information. At the higher levels, participle clauses make sentences shorter.

  • The woman who lives next door is a doctor.
  • My car, which is ten years old, still runs well.
  • Walking home, I met an old friend.
