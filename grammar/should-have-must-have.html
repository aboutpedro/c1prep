should have, could have, must have

A short explanation, then exercises from B1 to C2. Each exercise page has 5 sets of 10 sentences, a grammar guide, and every answer explained.

A modal + have + past participle talks about the past: regrets and criticism (should have), things that were possible but didn’t happen (could have), and guesses about what happened (must have, can’t have, might have).

  • I should have left earlier.
  • You could have told me!
  • She must have forgotten.
