Nouns and articles

A short explanation, then exercises from A1 to C1. Each exercise page has 5 sets of 10 sentences, a grammar guide, and every answer explained.

Use a or an for one of many, the when you both know which one, and no article for things in general. Plural nouns usually add -s, but some are irregular.

  • I saw a dog. The dog was huge.
  • Dogs are friendly.
  • one child, two children
