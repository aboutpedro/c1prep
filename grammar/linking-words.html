Linking words

A short explanation, then exercises from A2 to C2. Each exercise page has at least 5 sets of sentences to complete, a grammar guide, and every answer explained.

Linking words join ideas: and, but, so and because first, then although, despite, whereas, in order to and therefore. Watch what comes after each one: a clause, a noun or an -ing form.

  • I was tired, but I finished.
  • Although it was late, we went out.
  • Despite the rain, we went out.
