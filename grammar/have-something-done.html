have something done

A short explanation, then exercises from B1 to C2. Each exercise page has at least 5 sets of sentences to complete, a grammar guide, and every answer explained.

Have (or get) + object + past participle means that someone else does a job for you. It can also describe something bad that happened to you.

  • I’m having my hair cut tomorrow.
  • We got the car repaired.
  • He had his phone stolen.
