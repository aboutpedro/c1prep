Pronouns and possessives

A short explanation, then exercises from A1 to A2. Each exercise page has at least 5 sets of sentences to complete, a grammar guide, and every answer explained.

Subject pronouns (I, she) do the action; object pronouns (me, her) receive it. Possessive adjectives (my, her) go before a noun, and possessive pronouns (mine, hers) replace it. ’s shows that something belongs to someone.

  • She called me.
  • This is my bag. That one is yours.
  • Tom’s bike is red.
