Adjectives and adverbs

A short explanation, then exercises from A1 to C2. Each exercise page has 5 sets of 10 sentences, a grammar guide, and every answer explained.

Adjectives describe nouns; adverbs describe verbs, adjectives and whole sentences. Adverbs of frequency (always, often, never) usually go before the main verb. Adjectives ending in -ed say how you feel; those ending in -ing describe what causes the feeling.

  • She speaks slowly.
  • I usually walk to work.
  • The film was boring, so I was bored.
