The verb be

A short explanation, then exercises from A1 to A2. Each exercise page has at least 5 sets of sentences to complete, a grammar guide, and every answer explained.

Be has three forms in the present: am, is and are. We use it to say who or what someone is, where they are, how they feel and how old they are. For negatives, add not; for questions, put the verb before the subject.

  • I’m Ana. I’m from Brazil.
  • Is she at home? No, she isn’t.
  • They’re twelve.
