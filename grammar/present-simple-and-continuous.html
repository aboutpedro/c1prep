Present simple and present continuous

A short explanation, then exercises from A1 to C1. Each exercise page has 5 sets of 10 sentences, a grammar guide, and every answer explained.

The present simple is for habits, routines and things that are always true. The present continuous is for things happening now or around now, and for temporary situations. State verbs like know, like and understand usually stay in the simple form.

  • She works in a bank.
  • She’s working from home this week.
  • I don’t understand the question.
