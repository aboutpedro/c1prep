Reported speech

A short explanation, then exercises from B1 to C1. Each exercise page has 5 sets of 10 sentences, a grammar guide, and every answer explained.

When we report what someone said, the tense often moves one step back, and words like here, today and tomorrow change. Higher levels use reporting verbs such as suggest, deny and admit, each with its own pattern.

  • “I’m tired.” → She said she was tired.
  • He asked me where I lived.
  • She denied taking the money.
