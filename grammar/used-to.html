used to, would, be used to and get used to

A short explanation, then exercises from B1 to B2. Each exercise page has 5 sets of 10 sentences, a grammar guide, and every answer explained.

Used to + verb talks about past habits and states that are no longer true. Would can also describe past habits. Be used to and get used to + -ing mean that something is, or is becoming, normal for you.

  • I used to play the piano.
  • Every summer we would go to the beach.
  • I’m used to getting up early.
