Questions

A short explanation, then exercises from A1 to B2. Each exercise page has 5 sets of 10 sentences, a grammar guide, and every answer explained.

In most questions, the auxiliary (do, be, have, can…) comes before the subject. When who or what is the subject, there is no do. Indirect questions and question tags are used to be polite or to check something.

  • Where do you live?
  • Who called you?
  • It’s cold, isn’t it?
