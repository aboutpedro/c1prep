Past tenses

A short explanation, then exercises from A1 to C1. Each exercise page has at least 5 sets of sentences to complete, a grammar guide, and every answer explained.

The past simple tells the main events of a finished past. The past continuous sets the scene: what was in progress. The past perfect goes further back, to something that happened before another past event.

  • We didn’t go out last night.
  • I was walking home when it started to rain.
  • When we arrived, the film had already started.
