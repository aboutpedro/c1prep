The passive

A short explanation, then exercises from A2 to C2. Each exercise page has at least 5 sets of sentences to complete, a grammar guide, and every answer explained.

The passive (be + past participle) puts the focus on what happens to something, not on who does it. We use it when the person doing the action is unknown, obvious or not important. Every tense has a passive form.

  • This bridge was built in 1890.
  • English is spoken here.
  • The results will be announced tomorrow.
