Conditionals

A short explanation, then exercises from A2 to C2. Each exercise page has 5 sets of 10 sentences, a grammar guide, and every answer explained.

Conditionals talk about a condition and its result. The zero and first conditionals are about real situations; the second is about imaginary ones now; the third is about an imaginary past. Mixed conditionals and inversion come at the higher levels.

  • If you heat ice, it melts.
  • If it rains, we’ll stay in.
  • If I had known, I would have called you.
