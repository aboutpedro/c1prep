Comparison and degree

A short explanation, then exercises from A2 to C2. Each exercise page has at least 5 sets of sentences to complete, a grammar guide, and every answer explained.

Comparatives (bigger, more interesting) compare two things; superlatives (the biggest, the most interesting) compare one with the whole group. Too, enough, so and such say how much. Higher levels show how big the difference is.

  • This phone is cheaper than that one.
  • It’s the best film I’ve ever seen.
  • It’s far more expensive now.
