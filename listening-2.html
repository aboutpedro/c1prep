Listening · Part 2

Sentence completion

You will hear a sound designer called Owen Price talking about his work on nature documentaries. For questions 7–14, complete the sentences with a word or short phrase.

One speaker talks for about three minutes. You complete sentences with words you hear. You hear it twice.

How to do it

  1. Read all the sentences before the recording starts and think about what kind of word goes in each gap: a number, a noun, a place, an activity.
  2. The answers come in the same order as the sentences.
  3. Write the words you actually hear. You don't need to change their form.
  4. Use the second listening to fill any gaps and check your spelling.

Watch out for

  • The speaker often mentions something close to the answer first (another number, another place). Listen to the end of the idea before you write.
  • Spelling counts: an answer spelled wrongly is marked wrong.
  • Answers are usually one to three words. If yours is longer, check the sentence again.

In the C1 Advanced exam, the Listening paper takes about 40 minutes, and you hear every recording twice. You can replay recordings here, but try each set the exam way first.
