About

Future Perfect English is a place to practise English, from your first words at A1 to C2 Proficiency. Every exercise marks itself and explains every answer, so you learn from each mistake as you make it.

Who makes it

I’m Pedro Souza, an English teacher in Curitiba, Brazil. I teach teenagers and adults, and I prepare students for Cambridge English exams and the SAT. I was also Head Teacher of Debate and Model United Nations at Deutsche Schule Curitiba.

This site began in 2026 as a set of C1 Advanced exercises for my own students. They used it so much that it grew. Today it has practice for every level from A1 to C2, exam-style tasks for B2 First, C1 Advanced and C2 Proficiency, a level test, word games and a section for children.

How it works

  • No sign-up. Choose your level and start. There are no accounts and no ads.
  • Every answer explained. Press Check answers to see your score, the right answers and why they are right.
  • Your progress stays with you. It is saved in your browser. Move my progress takes it to another phone or computer.
  • Original material. Every text, task and explanation was written for this site. Questions from real exam papers are never copied.

The name

The future perfect is the tense of goals reached: by the time you sit your exam, you will have practised every part of it. The logo says the same thing. The top of the F is an arrow pointing forward.

Get in touch

Found a mistake in a set? Use Report a problem under it, and I’ll correct it. For anything else, such as questions, ideas, or using the site with your own students, write to pedrosouza@outlook.com.
